Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🙏thankful(adj.)
感激的▾
Simple meaning
feeling happy and grateful for something
Example
Zion is thankful for music.
➰curly(adj.)
卷曲的▾
Simple meaning
having curls or twists, like curly hair
Example
Kalina is thankful for her curly hair.
🎵music(n.)
音乐▾
Simple meaning
sounds, like songs, that are nice to listen to
Example
Zion is thankful for music.
🚂trains(n.)
火车▾
Simple meaning
long vehicles that run on tracks and carry people or things
Example
Tyler is thankful for trains.
👉show(v.)
展示▾
Simple meaning
to let people see something
Example
Some kids show what makes them thankful.
🧳trips(n.)
旅行▾
Simple meaning
journeys to visit a place
Example
Morgan is thankful for family trips.
👨‍👩‍👧family(n.)
家庭▾
Simple meaning
a group of people related to each other, like parents and children
Example
Melania is thankful for her family.
🏫school(n.)
学校▾
Simple meaning
a place where children go to learn
Example
Jack is thankful for school.
👦brother(n.)
兄弟▾
Simple meaning
a boy or man who has the same parents as you
Example
Rylee is thankful for her brother.
🐶dog(n.)
狗▾
Simple meaning
a common pet animal that barks
Example
Romeo is thankful for his dog.

Speaking

Practice speaking out loud! 大声说出来！

🟢 Level 1 — Simple Answers

🎤Is Zion thankful for music?

🎤Does Romeo have a dog?

🟡 Level 2 — Short Sentence Answers

🎤What is Tyler thankful for, and why do you think trains are fun?
🎤Name two kids from the book and what each one is thankful for.
🎤What does the word "thankful" mean?

🔵 Level 3 — Open-Ended Questions

🎤What are you thankful for, and why?
🎤Do you think it is important to say "thank you"? Why or why not?
🎤How can you show someone that you are thankful for them?
Useful phrases: ___ is thankful for ___. | I am thankful for... | What makes you thankful?

Wh- questions: "What makes you thankful?"
