Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🏃exercise(v./n.)
运动，锻炼▾
Simple meaning
to move your body to work hard and stay healthy
Example
Exercise is when you push your body to work hard.
💧sweat(v./n.)
出汗▾
Simple meaning
the water that comes out of your skin when you are hot or working hard
Example
Exercise makes you sweat.
❤️heart(n.)
心脏▾
Simple meaning
the part inside your body that beats and moves blood
Example
It makes your heart beat fast.
💨breathe(v.)
呼吸▾
Simple meaning
to take air into your body and let it out
Example
It makes you breathe quicker.
😊mood(n.)
心情▾
Simple meaning
the way you feel, like happy or sad
Example
Exercise can help your mood.
💪strong(adj.)
强壮的▾
Simple meaning
having a lot of power in your body
Example
Exercise can make you strong.
🍞hungry(adj.)
饥饿的▾
Simple meaning
wanting to eat food
Example
Exercise can make you hungry.
😴tired(adj.)
疲惫的▾
Simple meaning
needing to rest because you have no more energy
Example
Exercise can make you tired.
🤸stretch(v.)
伸展▾
Simple meaning
to make your arms or legs long and straight to loosen your body
Example
Remember to stretch before and after you exercise.
🥗healthy(adj.)
健康的▾
Simple meaning
well and not sick; feeling good in your body
Example
Exercise helps you stay healthy.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

You are exercising when you ___.
Exercise can make you ___.
Exercise can help you ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Present simple tense for facts — "Exercise makes you sweat."
"When" clauses for time/condition — "You are exercising when you ride a bicycle."
Modal "can" for ability/possibility — "Exercise can make you strong."

💭 Things to Remember

There are many ways to exercise: riding a bike, swimming, dancing, and running.
Exercise changes how your body feels — it makes you sweat, breathe faster, and your heart beat fast.
Remember to stretch and drink water before and after you exercise to stay healthy.
