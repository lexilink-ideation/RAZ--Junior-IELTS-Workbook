📐 Key Grammar

Simple past tense: "landed" and "flew" show actions that already happened. Example: "One crow landed on the wire."
Singular vs. plural nouns: add -s for more than one. Example: "Two crows landed on the wire."
Quantity adjectives "another," "more," and "all" tell us how many. Example: "More crows landed on the wire."

💭 Things to Remember

The crows landed on the wire one at a time.
More and more crows joined until the wire was full.
At the end, all the crows flew away together.
