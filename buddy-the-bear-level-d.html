🧵fall apart(v. phrase)

Simple meaning
to break into pieces or come undone
Example

❓lost(v.)

Simple meaning
past tense of "lose"; no longer having something
Example

💪arm(n.)
手臂▾
Simple meaning
the part of the body (or a toy) that reaches out from the shoulder
Example
Buddy the Bear lost an arm.
👂ear(n.)
耳朵▾
Simple meaning

Example
Buddy the Bear lost an ear.
👁️eye(n.)

Simple meaning

Example

🔄different(adj.)
不同的▾
Simple meaning

Example

❤️loved(v.)

Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.Buddy the Bear was a brand-new teddy bear.
2.Buddy the Bear lost an arm.
3.Buddy the Bear lost a leg in the story.
4.The family gave Buddy the Bear new parts when he lost old ones.
5.The family stopped loving Buddy the Bear when he looked different.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.What kind of toy is Buddy?
2.What happened to Buddy first in the story?
3.What did Buddy lose first?
4.What did the family do each time Buddy lost a part?
5.How did the family feel about Buddy at the end of the story?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.Why did Buddy the Bear begin to fall apart?

Hint: Think about how old the teddy bear was and how much he was hugged and played with.
2.What parts did Buddy the Bear lose in the story?

Hint: Think about his arm, his ear, and his eye.
3.Do you have a favorite toy? What would you do if it began to fall apart?

📐 Key Grammar

Simple Past Tense（一般过去时）: "Buddy the Bear lost an arm."
Adjective + Noun（形容词+名词）: "an old teddy bear"
Comparison with "before"（与从前比较）: "Buddy the Bear looked different than before."

💭 Things to Remember

Old toys can fall apart, but they can be fixed with new parts.
Losing a part doesn't change how much we love someone or something.
Buddy the Bear looked different at the end, but the family loved him just as much.
