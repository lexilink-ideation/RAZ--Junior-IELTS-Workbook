🙈hide(v.)
躲藏▾
Simple meaning
to go where no one can see you
Example

🪑behind(prep.)
在……后面▾
Simple meaning
at the back of something
Example

📦inside(prep.)
在……里面▾
Simple meaning
in something, not outside it
Example

🛏️covers(n.)

😴sleepy(adj.)

⏰time(n.)

🥱tired(adj.)

Example

💺chair(n.)

🧺basket(n.)

🚪closet(n.)

📐 Key Grammar

Present tense "I hide" describes what she does every night.
Prepositions "behind" and "inside" show where she hides.
Adjectives "sleepy" and "tired" describe how she feels.

💭 Things to Remember

The girl hides instead of going to bed because she is not sleepy.
She hides near six different family members (and the pet, Puff).
She finally gets tired and hides under the covers to sleep.
