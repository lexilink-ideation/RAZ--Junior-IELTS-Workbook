A · True or False 判断对错

2.They get balloons at the bakery.
3.They get a cake at the bakery.
4.They get a toy at the ice cream shop.

1.Where do they get a card?
2.Where do they get balloons?
3.What do they get at the bakery?

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present "go/get/play/sing" — daily actions. Ex: "We go shopping."
Plural nouns with "-s": balloons, games.
Pronoun "we" — shows the children act together.

💭 Things to Remember
