📐 Key Grammar

Simple Present Tense (一般现在时): describes facts — "We have scales."
Plural Nouns (复数名词): most coverings are plural — scales, feathers, spines.
Subject-Verb Agreement (主谓一致): "We have" vs. "It has."

💭 Things to Remember

Animals have many different kinds of body coverings.
Coverings protect animals and help them survive.
Some animals, like a bald man, have no hair — just skin.
