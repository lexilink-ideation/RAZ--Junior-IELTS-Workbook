Simple meaning
better than everyone or everything else

👍better(adj.)
更好的▾
Simple meaning
more good than something else
Example
"I am better," said Bear.
🎣catch(v.)
抓住▾
Simple meaning
to grab something that is moving
Example
"I can catch fish," said Kangaroo.
🧗climb(v.)
爬▾
Simple meaning
to move up something using your hands and feet
Example
"I can climb up high," said Kangaroo.
🤹juggle(v.)
玩杂耍▾
Simple meaning
to throw and catch several things at the same time
Example
"I can juggle three balls," said Kangaroo.
💪lift(v.)
举起▾
Simple meaning
to pick something up and raise it
Example
"I can lift a goat," said Kangaroo.
🦘hop(v.)
跳▾
Simple meaning
to jump on one or both feet
Example
"I can hop high," said Kangaroo.
🐐goat(n.)
山羊▾
Simple meaning
a farm animal with horns that likes to climb
Example
"I can lift six goats," said Bear.
🫐berries(n.)
浆果▾
Simple meaning
small, round, juicy fruits
Example
"I can eat more berries," said Bear.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

"I can ___," said Kangaroo.
"I can ___ more/better," said Bear.
"I can, too," said Bear.

📐 Key Grammar

Comparative adjectives: best vs. better ("I am the best" / "I am better").
Modal verb "can" for ability: "I can catch fish."
Direct speech with quotation marks and "said + name."

💭 Things to Remember

Bear and Kangaroo are friends who like to compete in a fun way.
Each time, Bear tries to do a little better than Kangaroo.
At the end, they realize they can both do the same thing.
