🧩 Sentence Patterns

I like to ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense: subject + verb — "I read."
Idiom "all by myself" means alone, without any help.
The pronoun "I" pairs with the base verb (no -s) — "I read," "I play," not "reads/plays."

💭 Things to Remember

The girl can do many things without anyone's help: reading, coloring, singing, playing, building, listening, dressing, and thinking.
Learning to do things all by yourself is an exciting part of growing up.
Doing things independently can make you feel proud and confident.
