🧩 Sentence Patterns

These bees need a ___.
These bees look in the ___.
___ is where the heart is.

📐 Key Grammar

“These” + plural noun: these bees (more than one).
Present simple with plural subject: bees need, bees look (no -s).
“in the ___” shows place: in the log, in the mailbox.

💭 Things to Remember

Bees need a safe home.
Many places are not good homes for bees.
