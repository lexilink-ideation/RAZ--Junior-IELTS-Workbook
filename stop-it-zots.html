🧩 Sentence Patterns

Zots ___ in the ___.
"Then ___," Abby says.

📐 Key Grammar

Quotation marks show a character's exact words — e.g. "I'm bored," Zots says.
Prepositional phrases show where something happens — e.g. Zots bounces in the bedroom.
Simple present tense narrates repeated actions in a story — e.g. Zots races the dog in the hallway.

💭 Things to Remember
