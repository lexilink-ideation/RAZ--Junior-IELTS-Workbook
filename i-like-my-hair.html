Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🌀curly(adj.)
卷曲的▾
Simple meaning
shaped in rings or waves; not straight
Example
My hair is curly. I like to let it grow.
➡️straight(adj.)
直的▾
Simple meaning
going in one direction without bending or curling
Example
My hair is straight. It swings when I walk.
🌊swing(v.)
摆动▾
Simple meaning
to move back and forth smoothly
Example
I like my hair to swing when I walk my dog.
🟠freckles(n.)
雀斑▾
Simple meaning
small brown or red spots on the skin
Example
My red hair matches my freckles perfectly.
🪢braids(n.)
辫子▾
Simple meaning
hair twisted or woven together into a rope shape
Example
My hair has braids. I like to swing them when I play.
💈spikes(n.)
尖刺状发型▾
Simple meaning
stiff points of hair sticking up
Example
My hair has spikes. I like to paint them in colors.
🎨paint(v.)
涂色▾
Simple meaning
to put color on something
Example
I like to paint my spikes in bright colors.
🔀different(adj.)
不同的▾
Simple meaning
not the same as others; not alike
Example
We can wear our hair in lots of different ways.
⬇️hang(v.)
悬挂▾
Simple meaning
to fall downward; to be suspended
Example
My hair is long. I like it to hang upside down.
✂️cut(v./adj.)
剪短▾
Simple meaning
to use scissors to make something shorter
Example
My mom likes my curly hair cut short.

Speaking

🟢 Level 1 — Simple Answers

🎤Is your hair curly or straight?
🎤Is your hair long or short?
🎤What color is your hair?

🟡 Level 2 — Short Sentence Answers

🎤Tell me about one hair style from the book. Use "My hair is ___."
🎤What do braids look like? Describe them in a sentence.
🎤Why do you think the child likes their hair? Use "I think the child likes their hair because ___."

🔵 Level 3 — Open-Ended Questions

🎤The book says we can wear hair in many different ways. Can you think of other hair styles not in the book?
🎤Why is it good that people have different hair styles?
🎤If you could have any hair style in the world, what would it be? Why?
Useful phrases: My hair is ___ | I like my hair to ___ | Hair can be ___ | I think ___ is fun

📐 Key Grammar

Verb + infinitive: I like to let it grow.
When-clause: I like it to swing when I walk.

💭 Things to Remember

People can have many different hair types and styles.
Hair styles: curly, straight, braids, spikes, short, long.
Everyone is unique — differences are wonderful!
