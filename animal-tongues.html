Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
👅tongue(n.)
舌头▾
Simple meaning
the soft part in the mouth used to taste and eat
Example
Animals have many kinds of tongues.
🦣anteater(n.)
食蚁兽▾
Simple meaning
an animal that eats ants with a long tongue
Example
This anteater has a long, thin tongue.
🦎lizard(n.)
蜥蜴▾
Simple meaning
a small reptile with a long tail
Example
This lizard has a tongue with two parts.
🦒giraffe(n.)
长颈鹿▾
Simple meaning
a tall animal with a very long neck
Example
This giraffe has a dark, strong tongue.
🐸frog(n.)
青蛙▾
Simple meaning
a small animal that hops and catches bugs
Example
This frog has a long, sticky tongue.
📏long(adj.)
长的▾
Simple meaning
having a large length; not short
Example
This anteater has a long tongue.
🫧thin(adj.)
细的▾
Simple meaning
narrow; not wide or thick
Example
This anteater has a long, thin tongue.
🍯sticky(adj.)
粘的▾
Simple meaning
able to stick to things
Example
This frog has a sticky tongue.
💪strong(adj.)
有力的▾
Simple meaning
having a lot of power
Example
This giraffe has a strong tongue.
🍽️food(n.)
食物▾
Simple meaning
things that animals eat
Example
They use their tongues to eat food.

have/has: Animals have, this frog has.
Infinitive of purpose: to get and eat food.
