Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🐹hamster(n.)
仓鼠▾
Simple meaning
a small, furry animal often kept as a pet
Example
James has a hamster named Hank who lives in a cage.
🏠cage(n.)
笼子▾
Simple meaning
a box with bars where an animal lives
Example
James wanted to buy a new cage for his hamster Hank.
🔢count(v.)
数数▾
Simple meaning
to add up numbers to find a total
Example
James counted his money — he had fourteen dollars.
💰earn(v.)
赚取▾
Simple meaning
to receive money in return for work
Example
"I'll need to earn the money," James said.
🍂rake(v.)
耙叶子▾
Simple meaning
to gather fallen leaves using a garden tool
Example
Grandpa paid James two dollars an hour to rake leaves.
🚗garage(n.)
车库▾
Simple meaning
a building where you park a car or store things
Example
Mom paid James three dollars an hour to clean the garage.
🌿weeds(n.)
杂草▾
Simple meaning
unwanted plants that grow where they are not wanted
Example
Mrs. Tom paid James four dollars an hour to pull weeds.
😊proud(adj.)
自豪的▾
Simple meaning
feeling happy and satisfied about something you did
Example
James was proud of his hard work after earning the money.
💵cost(v.)
花费▾
Simple meaning
to have a price of a certain amount
Example
"The cage costs thirty dollars," said James.
⏰hour(n.)
小时▾
Simple meaning
a unit of time equal to 60 minutes
Example
James raked leaves for three hours to earn money.

James saw a cool new hamster cage. "Hamster Hank would like this," he said. "Hank could run in all the tubes and rooms."
💰 James sitting at a table counting coins and dollar bills carefully
Pages 5–6
At home, James counted his money. "I have fourteen dollars," said James. "The cage costs thirty dollars."

🍂 Grandpa paying James; Mom showing James the garage to clean
Pages 7–8
"I'll pay you two dollars an hour to rake leaves," said Grandpa.
"I'll pay you three dollars an hour to clean the garage," said Mom.
🌿 James pulling weeds in Mrs. Tom's garden; a calendar shows the hours he worked
Pages 9–10
"I'll pay you four dollars an hour to pull these weeds," said Mrs. Tom.
James pulled weeds for one hour. He cleaned the garage for two hours. He raked leaves for three hours.
🎉 James holding the new hamster cage with a big smile; Hank the hamster inside his new home
Pages 11–12
"Now I have the money I need," James said. "I can buy Hank's new cage!"
James got Hank's new cage. He was proud of his hard work. Hank was proud of his new home!

Speaking

Practice speaking out loud! 大声说出来！

🟢 Level 1 — Simple Answers

🎤What is the name of James's hamster?
🎤How much did the cage cost?
🎤Did James earn his money or ask for a gift?

🟡 Level 2 — Short Sentence Answers

🎤Tell me the three jobs James did. Use "James ___ed ..."
🎤Why did James feel proud? Use "James felt proud because ___."
🎤Who paid James the most per hour? Use "Mrs. Tom paid James ___ dollars an hour."

🔵 Level 3 — Open-Ended Questions

🎤James worked hard to earn money. Why is it important to earn what you want instead of just asking for it?
🎤If you needed to earn money, what job would you do? Why?
🎤James did math to figure out how much he could earn. Why is math useful in real life?
Useful phrases: James earned ___ | He worked for ___ hours | I'll pay you ___ to ___ | James was proud of ___
