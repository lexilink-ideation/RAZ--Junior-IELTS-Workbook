Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🚗traffic(n.)
交通▾
Simple meaning
cars, buses, trucks, and other vehicles moving on a road
Example
You must look for traffic before crossing the street.
🚶cross(v.)
穿越▾
Simple meaning
to move from one side of something to the other side
Example
You must always cross the street safely.
🛡️safely(adv.)
安全地▾
Simple meaning
in a way that avoids danger or risk
Example
Always cross the street safely — look both ways!
🛑curb(n.)
路缘石▾
Simple meaning
the raised edge of the sidewalk next to the road
Example
Wait on the curb of the sidewalk until it is safe to cross.
🚦signal(n.)
信号灯▾
Simple meaning
a light or sound that tells you when it is safe to cross
Example
You can cross when the signal shows the word WALK.
🦓crosswalk(n.)
人行横道▾
Simple meaning
marked lines on the road where people walk across safely
Example
Cross inside the lines of a crosswalk whenever possible.
🎮control(v.)
控制▾
Simple meaning
to make something do what you want; to manage or direct
Example
Stop signs control traffic at some street corners.
💡flashing(adj.)
闪烁的▾
Simple meaning
turning on and off quickly, like a blinking light
Example
Be careful if the signal is flashing — the light will change soon.
🧭corner(n.)
街角▾
Simple meaning
the place where two roads or streets meet
Example
Cross the street at the corner of a block or a marked crossing.
🔍lane(n.)
车道▾
Simple meaning
one section of a road where vehicles travel
Example
Look at the lane closest to you last — that's where traffic is nearest.

Story

Read the story. Target words are highlighted. 多读几次！
🚸 A crossing guard stopping traffic so students can safely cross the street to school
Pages 3–6 · Introduction
Do you ever walk to school or the park? You might have to cross the street to get there.
Some streets are busy with traffic. You must always cross the street safely.
🛑 A child stopping at a street corner and looking both ways for traffic
Pages 7–8 · How to Cross
It is a good idea to cross the street with a grown-up. Before you cross, stop at the street corner. Wait on the curb of the sidewalk until it is safe. Look for traffic. Make sure to look both ways. Look at the lane closest to you last. You can cross when there are no cars or when all cars stop. Do not run, but quickly cross the street.
🚦 A pedestrian traffic signal showing WALK; then another showing a red hand — DON'T WALK
Pages 9–10 · Signals
Some street corners have signals that tell people when to cross. You can cross when the signal shows WALK or a walking person. Never cross when the signal shows DON'T WALK or a red hand.
Some streets have a crosswalk. Cross inside the lines of a crosswalk.
🔴 A traffic light at a street corner; a stop sign; parked cars blocking the view
Pages 11–12
Be careful if the signal is flashing. The light will soon turn green for cars. Some street corners have stop signs to control traffic. Wait at the corner for all traffic to stop. Make sure parked cars don't block your view.
🌈 A child in bright clothing crossing the street safely; a smiling crossing guard nearby
Pages 13–15 · Stay Safe
Wearing bright colors helps drivers see you. Look both ways. Look at the lane closest to you last. Watch out for cars turning into your path. Traffic is more than just cars — watch out for trucks, buses, motorcycles, and bicycles too.
Every time you cross the street, cross safely.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

You must always cross the street ___.
You can cross when the signal shows ___.
Look at the lane closest to you ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Modal "must": You must always cross safely.
Conditional: You can cross when there are no cars.
Imperative: Look both ways. Stop at the corner.

💭 Things to Remember

Always cross at a corner or crosswalk — never in the middle.
Look both ways and check the lane closest to you last.
Only cross when the signal says WALK or all cars stop.
