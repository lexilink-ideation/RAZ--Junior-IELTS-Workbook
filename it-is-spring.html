📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense: verb + s — "The Sun pops up."
Plural nouns: add -s — "The flowers pop out."
Adverbs of place — "Spring pops out everywhere."

💭 Things to Remember

Spring is the season when new things grow and appear.
The sun, plants, worms, leaves, birds, and animals all pop up or pop out in spring.
Spring changes the world all around us — pop, pop, pop!
