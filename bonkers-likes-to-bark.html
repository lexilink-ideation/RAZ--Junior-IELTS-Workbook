🧩 Sentence Patterns

___ likes to bark at ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present + "-s" for he/she/it: Bonkers likes to bark.
"bark at" + noun shows the target: Bonkers likes to bark at cats.
Plural nouns end in "-s": cars, cats, bikes, squirrels, kids, birds.

💭 Things to Remember

Bonkers is a dog who loves to bark at many things.
He barks at cars, cats, bikes, squirrels, kids, and birds.
