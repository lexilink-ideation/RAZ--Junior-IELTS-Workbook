Vocabulary

🌅wake up(v.)
醒来▾
Simple meaning
to stop sleeping and open your eyes
Example
Lily the cat woke up from her nap.
😴nap(n.)
小睡▾
Simple meaning
a short sleep, often during the day
Example
Lily the cat woke up from her nap.
⬇️jump down(v.)
跳下来▾
Simple meaning
to jump from a higher place to a lower place
Example
She jumped down from the bed.
🚪walk through(v.)
走过；穿过▾
Simple meaning
to walk from one side of something to the other side
Example
She walked through the door.
🚶cross(v.)
穿过；横过▾
Simple meaning
to go from one side of a space to the other side
Example
She crossed the room.
🔄circle(v.)
绕圈走▾
Simple meaning
to move around something in the shape of a circle
Example
She circled the chair.
🐾stroll(v.)
漫步；闲逛▾
Simple meaning
to walk slowly and calmly, without hurrying
Example
She strolled under the table.
🐈curl up(v.)
蜷缩▾
Simple meaning
to bend your body into a small, round, comfortable shape
Example
Lily curled up on Jack's lap to nap.
🛋️couch(n.)
沙发▾
Simple meaning
a long, soft seat that more than one person can sit on
Example
She saw the couch.
🧎lap(n.)
大腿；膝上▾
Simple meaning
the flat area formed by your thighs when you are sitting down
Example
She saw Jack's lap.

📐 Key Grammar

Regular past tense (-ed): walked, jumped, crossed, circled, strolled, curled — e.g. "She walked through the door."
Irregular past tense "saw" (see → saw) — e.g. "She saw a door."
Prepositions of place: from, through, under, on — e.g. "She strolled under the table."

💭 Things to Remember

Lily is a cat who explores different rooms and objects in her house.
Lily moves from the bedroom, through a door, across a room, past a chair and table, to the couch.
At the end of the story, Lily curls up on Jack's lap to nap, just like she began the story napping.
