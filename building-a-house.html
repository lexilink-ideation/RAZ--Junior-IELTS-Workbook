🏗️ Builders pouring a concrete foundation for a new house

Houses do not all look the same, but most home builders follow the same basic steps. First, the builders make a strong base for the house. The base is called the foundation.
🏠 Workers building wooden wall frames and a roof on a house
Page 5-6
Next, the builders add the floors, walls, and roof. Then, the builders cover the roof and outside walls to keep the rain out. They leave the inside walls open so things can go inside them.
🔧 A worker installing wires and pipes inside the wall frame
Page 7-8
Next, the builders put in the windows and doors. Then, the builders put wires and pipes inside the walls.
🧱 Builders adding insulation and closing up the walls with panels
Page 9-10
Next, the builders fill the empty spaces inside the walls and under the roof. This helps keep the house warm in winter and cool in summer. Then, the builders close up the walls and ceilings. Now the floors can be covered and the walls can be painted.
💡 A finished kitchen with lights, switches, and cabinets installed
Page 11-12
Next, the builders put in all the lights and light switches. Finally, the builders put in the kitchen and the bathrooms. Now, the house is ready to become someone's home!

🧩 Sentence Patterns

First, the builders ___.

Finally, the builders ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Sequence adverbs (First, Next, Then, Finally) show the order of steps: "First, the builders make a strong base... Next, the builders add the floors, walls, and roof."
Simple present tense describes a general process: "The builders make a strong base for the house."
"Is called" names something: "The base is called the foundation."

💭 Things to Remember

The foundation is the strong base of the house, and it is built first.
Wires and pipes go inside the walls before the walls are closed up and painted.
Switches and lights are added near the end, and the kitchen and bathrooms come last.
