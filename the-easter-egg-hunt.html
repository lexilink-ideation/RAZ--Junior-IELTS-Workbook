Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.The children look for Easter eggs.
2.They try to find seven eggs.
3.The first egg is in the pot.
4.The last egg is under the dog.
5.The story happens at night in winter.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.What holiday is it in the story?
2.How many eggs do they want to find?
3.Where is the first egg?
4.Where is the last (seventh) egg?
5.What are the children doing?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.Where do the children find the first egg?

Hint: Look up high in something tall and green.
2.How many eggs are hidden in the backyard?

Hint: It is the number after six.
3.Where would you hide an Easter egg in your home?
