Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🐘elephant(n.)
大象▾
Simple meaning
a very big gray animal with a long nose called a trunk
Example
The elephant uses its long trunk to spray water.
🚿washes(v.)
洗▾
Simple meaning
cleans something using water
Example
The elephant washes the car with lots of water.
⬅️front(n.)
前面▾
Simple meaning
the part of something that faces forward
Example
The headlights are at the front of the car.
➡️back(n.)
后面▾
Simple meaning
the part of something that is behind or at the rear
Example
The trunk of the car is at the back.
🔝top(n.)
顶部▾
Simple meaning
the highest part of something
Example
The elephant can reach the top of the car with its trunk.
🚪door(n.)
门▾
Simple meaning
a flat part you open to get in or out of the car
Example
Open the door and get in the car!
🔵tire(n.)
轮胎▾
Simple meaning
the round black rubber ring that goes around a wheel
Example
Each tire on the car is big and round.
🪟window(n.)
窗户▾
Simple meaning
the clear glass part of a car you can see through
Example
The elephant cleans the window until it is very clear.
🧑‍✈️driver(n.)
司机▾
Simple meaning
the person who sits in the car and makes it move
Example
The driver got very wet — what a surprise!
😄laughs(v.)
笑▾
Simple meaning
makes a happy sound when something is funny
Example
The elephant laughs because washing the driver was very funny!

🧩 Sentence Patterns

The elephant washes the ___ of the car.
The elephant laughs!
The ___ is at the front / back / top.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple Present: "washes" — an action the elephant does now
Article "the": the front, the tire, the driver — specific things
Preposition "of": "of the car" — shows what part belongs to

💭 Things to Remember

An elephant is a huge animal with a long trunk!
A car has many parts: front, back, top, door, tire, window.
The elephant washes the driver too — that's the funny surprise!
