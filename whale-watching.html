Vocabulary

🤔tricky(adj.)
棘手的▾
Simple meaning
not easy to do; difficult
Example
Watching for whales is tricky.
🧐carefully(adv.)
仔细地▾
Simple meaning
paying close attention; in a careful way
Example
I look carefully to spot them.
👀spot(v.)
发现；看到▾
Simple meaning
to see or notice something
Example
I spot three big shapes.
⚓docks(n.)
码头▾
Simple meaning
a place by the water where boats stop
Example
I watch for whales by the docks.
💧surface(n.)
表面；水面▾
Simple meaning
the top of the water
Example
I watch for whales near the surface.
🌊waves(n.)
波浪▾
Simple meaning
moving lines of water in the sea
Example
I watch for whales in the waves.
🔭watch(v.)
观察；看▾
Simple meaning
to look at something for a while
Example
I watch for whales out at sea.
🍀luck(n.)
运气▾
Simple meaning
something good that happens by chance
Example
No luck.
🔷shapes(n.)
形状▾
Simple meaning
the outline or form of something
Example
I spot three big shapes.
🐋whales(n.)
鲸鱼▾
Simple meaning
very large animals that live in the ocean
Example
I watch three whales follow the fish.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple Present Tense（一般现在时）: "I watch for whales."
Prepositions of place（方位介词）: near, by, in, out at — "by the docks", "in the waves"
Infinitive of purpose（不定式表目的）: "I look carefully to spot them."

💭 Things to Remember

It is tricky to spot whales because the ocean is big.
The girl looks in many places: near the boat, at sea, by the docks, near the surface, and in the waves.
She finally spots the whales by first noticing the fish they follow.
