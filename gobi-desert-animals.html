🧩 Sentence Patterns

[Animal] live in the Gobi.
Many animals live in the Gobi Desert.
A [animal] is an animal that lives in the desert.

📐 Key Grammar

Plural nouns: add -s (camel→camels, eagle→eagles), but wolf→wolves (irregular).
Subject-verb agreement: plural subject + verb with no -s, e.g. "Camels live," "Eagles live."
Prepositional phrase of place: "in the Gobi" / "in the Gobi Desert" tells us where.

💭 Things to Remember

The Gobi Desert is in Asia and is home to many kinds of animals.
Camels, eagles, grasshoppers, horses, goats, wolves, and lizards all live in the Gobi.
A desert can be a home for many different animals, even though it looks empty.
