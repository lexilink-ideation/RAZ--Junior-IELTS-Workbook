🧩 Sentence Patterns

I like to ___ after school.
We like to have ___ after school.
I like to ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Subject + like + to + verb: "I like to study."
Base form verbs after "to": study, play, dance, act, read, sing, bike
"We" for a group: "We like to have fun."

💭 Things to Remember
