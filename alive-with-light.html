Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
💡light(n.)
光；光亮▾
Simple meaning
the bright thing that helps us see
Example
Light helps animals see the world around them.
🌱living(adj.)
有生命的▾
Simple meaning
alive; growing and breathing, not dead
Example
Some living things can make light on their own.
🧍bodies(n.)
身体▾
Simple meaning
more than one body; the whole of a person or animal
Example
People don't have bodies that give off light.
🎣catch(v.)
捕捉；抓住▾
Simple meaning
to get and hold something that is moving
Example
The light can help living things catch their food.
💞mates(n.)
配偶；伴侣▾
Simple meaning
two animals that come together to have babies
Example
The light can help living things find their mates.
😊enjoy(v.)
享受；喜欢▾
Simple meaning
to like doing something and feel happy
Example
People can enjoy seeing other things alive with light!
🛟stay(v.)
保持；停留▾
Simple meaning
to keep being the same way
Example
The light helps living things so they stay safe.
🛡️protect(v.)
保护▾
Simple meaning
to keep something safe from harm
Example
The light can help protect living things.
🪰firefly(n.)
萤火虫▾
Simple meaning
a small bug that makes yellow light at night
Example
A firefly sits on a leaf and makes yellow light.
🎐jellyfish(n.)
水母▾
Simple meaning
a soft sea animal; some kinds can glow
Example
Some jellyfish make blue light.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

Some living things make ___ light.
The light can help living things ___.
People don't have bodies that ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present for facts: "Light helps animals see the world around them."
can + verb (ability): "Some living things can make light on their own."
don't + verb (negative): "People don't have bodies that give off light."

💭 Things to Remember

Some living things make their own light — fireflies, jellyfish, glowworms and deep-sea fish.
The light can be blue, yellow, red or green.
Living things use light to catch food, find mates and stay safe.
