Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.Ted and Peg go on a picnic.
2.Peg gets a picnic basket.
3.Ted puts in sandwiches.
4.Peg puts in cookies.
5.A dog is in the basket at the end.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.Who gets the picnic basket?
2.What does Ted put in first?
3.What does Peg put in after the basket?
4.What is in the basket at the end of the story?
5.What does Ted put in last?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.What foods do Ted and Peg put in the basket? Name three.

Hint: Think of cheese, sandwiches, chips, drinks, apples, and cookies.
2.Why do you think the mouse got into the basket?

Hint: Think about what mice like to eat — there was lots of yummy food inside!
3.What would you bring on a picnic?

Speaking

🔵 Level 3 — Open-Ended Questions

🎤What would you pack for a picnic, and why?
🎤Why do you think the mouse got into the basket?

Useful phrases: Ted puts in... | Peg puts in... | I can see a... | There is a... in the basket.
