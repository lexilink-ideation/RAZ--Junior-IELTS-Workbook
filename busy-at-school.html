🧩 Sentence Patterns

They ___ a ___.
They ___ to a ___.
The children are busy at school.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense: "They sing a song." describes things that happen regularly.
Subject + verb + object: "They paint a picture."
Plural pronoun "they" stands for more than one child.

💭 Things to Remember

Children do many different activities at school every day.
Sharing and listening are important classroom habits.
The story ends by asking: what other things do they do?
