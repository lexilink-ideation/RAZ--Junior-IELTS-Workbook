🧩 Sentence Patterns

I read with my ___.
I read to my ___.
My ___ is a member of my family.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense: subject + verb — "I read..."
Possessive adjective "my" before a noun — "my mom," "my dad"
Preposition "with" (a person) vs. "to" (an animal) — "read with my sister" / "read to my dog"

💭 Things to Remember

The girl reads with many family members: mom, dad, grandma, grandpa, aunt, sister, and brother.
Reading can be shared with people you love, not just done alone.
Even a pet, like a dog, can be part of story time!
