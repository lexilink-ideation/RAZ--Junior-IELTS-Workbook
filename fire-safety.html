Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🔥fire(n.)
火▾
Simple meaning
the burning of something, with hot flames that can be dangerous
Example
House fires happen every day.
🚨smoke alarm(n.)
烟雾报警器▾
Simple meaning
a device on the ceiling that makes a loud sound when it senses smoke
Example
Your house should have smoke alarms.
🗺️escape plan(n.)
逃生计划▾
Simple meaning
a plan that shows how to get out safely and where to meet
Example
Practice an escape plan with your family's help.
🙈hide(v.)
躲藏▾
Simple meaning
to go somewhere so that no one can see or find you
Example
Never hide under your bed or in a closet.
🧎crawl(v.)
爬行▾
Simple meaning
to move along the ground on your hands and knees
Example
Crawl on the floor if there is a lot of smoke.
💨smoke(n.)
烟▾
Simple meaning
the gray or black cloud that rises from something burning
Example
They make a really loud sound when there's smoke.
🛡️safe(adj.)
安全的▾
Simple meaning
not in danger; protected from being hurt
Example
Go outside where it is safe.
🔥burn(v.)
燃烧▾
Simple meaning
to be on fire, or to be hurt by fire or heat
Example
If your clothes start to burn, don't panic!
😱panic(v.)
惊慌▾
Simple meaning
to suddenly feel so worried or scared that you can't think clearly
Example
Don't panic! Drop to the floor and roll.

Story

Read the story. Target words are highlighted. 多读几次！
🏠🔥 A house with flames and smoke coming out of a window while a fire truck arrives
Pages 3–4 · Fired Up!
House fires happen every day. House fires can hurt people. But you can help! Learn these tips to keep you and your family safe.
🚨 A smoke alarm on the ceiling flashing and beeping loudly, while a family points to a meeting spot outside on a map
Pages 5–6 · Smoke Alarms / Escape Plan
Your house should have smoke alarms. They make a really loud sound when there's smoke. Get out of the house when the alarm sounds. Practice an escape plan with your family's help. Pick a place outside to meet them.
🧎💨 A child crawling low along the floor toward a door, away from a bed and a closet
Pages 7–8 · Don't Hide, Go Outside / Stay Low
When there's a fire, never hide under your bed. Never hide in a closet. Go outside where it is safe. Crawl on the floor if there is a lot of smoke. It is easier to breathe near the floor.
🚪✋ A child touching a closed door with the back of a hand, and another child dropping to the floor to roll
Pages 9–10 · Touch and Go / Roll! Roll! Roll!
Touch the door before you go. (Don't touch the knob!) If the door feels hot, don't open it. There might be a fire on the other side. Find another way out. 1. Stop! 2. Drop! 3. Roll! If your clothes start to burn, don't panic! Drop to the floor. Roll around until the fire is out.
🚒☎️ A firefighter helping a family stand safely outside their house while someone calls 9-1-1 on a phone
Pages 11–12 · Get Out and Stay Out / Fire Safe
Never go back into a burning house. Stay out once you are out. Always call 9-1-1 if you smell smoke or if there is a fire. The fire department will help. Always keep these tips in mind. You will be safe if you do.

📐 Key Grammar

Imperatives for instructions: "Crawl on the floor." "Stop! Drop! Roll!"
"Should" for advice: "Your house should have smoke alarms."
Sequence words for order: "1. Stop! 2. Drop! 3. Roll!"

💭 Things to Remember

Get out fast when the smoke alarm sounds — don't hide!
Stay low and crawl under smoke; touch doors before opening them.
If clothes catch fire: Stop, Drop, Roll. Never go back inside — call 9-1-1.
