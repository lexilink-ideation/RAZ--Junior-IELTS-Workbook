🧩 Sentence Patterns

You can ride the ___.
What is your favorite ___?
The ___ is ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Modal verb "can" for possibility: "You can ride the big wheel."
Adjective before noun: "the painted horse," "the tiny plane."
Question form with "What is...?": "What is your favorite ride?"

💭 Things to Remember

Fairs have many different kinds of rides.
Some rides are big and slow, some are small and fast.
Everyone can have a different favorite ride.
