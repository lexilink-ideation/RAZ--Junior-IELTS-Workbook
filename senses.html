Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🧠senses(n.)
感官▾
Simple meaning
the five ways we learn about the world: seeing, hearing, smelling, touching, tasting
Example
I use my five senses to learn about things.
👀see(v.)
看见▾
Simple meaning
to use your eyes to notice things
Example
I use my eyes to see birds and bees.
👂hear(v.)
听▾
Simple meaning
to use your ears to notice sounds
Example
I use my ears to hear birds singing.
👃smell(v.)
闻▾
Simple meaning
to use your nose to notice a scent
Example
I use my nose to smell pretty flowers.
🤏touch(v.)
触摸▾
Simple meaning
to use your fingers to feel something
Example
I use my fingers to touch the fuzzy caterpillar.
👅taste(v.)
品尝▾
Simple meaning
to use your tongue to notice the flavor of food
Example
I use my tongue to taste sweet berries.
🐛fuzzy(adj.)
毛茸茸的▾
Simple meaning
covered in soft, fine hair or fibers
Example
The caterpillar is fuzzy. I like to touch it.
🍓sweet(adj.)
甜的▾
Simple meaning
having a pleasant sugary flavor; like candy or fruit
Example
The berries in the garden are sweet and delicious.
🌻garden(n.)
花园▾
Simple meaning
a place outside where plants, flowers, or vegetables grow
Example
I like to go to my garden. I can use all my senses there.
📚learn(v.)
学习▾
Simple meaning
to get new knowledge or skills
Example
I use my five senses to learn about things around me.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

I use my ___ to ___.
I ___ [birds / flowers / berries].
I learn a lot from my senses.

📐 Key Grammar

Verb + infinitive: I use my eyes to see.
Simple present: I hear birds singing.
Adjectives: fuzzy caterpillar, sweet berries

💭 Things to Remember

We have five senses: see, hear, smell, touch, taste.
Each sense uses a different body part.
We use our senses every day to learn about the world.
