🧩 Sentence Patterns

You can ___ to calm down.
Everyone feels angry sometimes.
It's okay if you feel angry.

📐 Key Grammar

Modal can + verb: You can breathe.
Infinitive of purpose: ___ to calm down.
“everyone” takes a singular verb: Everyone feels.

💭 Things to Remember

Everyone feels angry sometimes — that is okay.
There are many healthy ways to calm down: breathe, count, walk, sing, write, draw, think, talk.
Sharing your feelings helps you feel better.
