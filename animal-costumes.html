Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🎭costumes(n.)
服装（戏服）▾
Simple meaning
special clothes that make you look like someone or something else
Example
Animals can wear costumes too.
👕wear(v.)
穿；戴▾
Simple meaning
to have something on your body
Example
People wear costumes.
🪽wings(n.)
翅膀▾
Simple meaning
the parts that let a bird or bug fly
Example
A dog can wear wings.
🦁mane(n.)
鬃毛▾
Simple meaning
the long hair on the neck of an animal like a lion
Example
A dog can wear a mane.
👑crown(n.)
皇冠▾
Simple meaning
a special hat that kings and queens wear
Example
A goat can wear a crown.
🧣bandanna(n.)
头巾▾
Simple meaning
a small, colorful cloth tied around the neck or head
Example
A cat can wear a bandanna.
🥸mask(n.)
面具▾
Simple meaning
something that covers your face to hide it or change how you look
Example
A dog can wear a mask.
🐟fins(n.)
鱼鳍▾
Simple meaning
flat parts that help a fish move in the water
Example
A dog can wear fins.
🎩hat(n.)
帽子▾
Simple meaning
something you wear on your head
Example
A rabbit can wear a hat.
🐷snout(n.)
（动物的）鼻子▾
Simple meaning
the long nose of an animal, like a pig
Example
A dog can wear a snout.

1.What is a costume? Why do people and animals wear them in this book?

📐 Key Grammar

Modal verb "can" for ability: "A dog can wear wings."
Simple present tense for facts: "People wear costumes."
Wh-question with present continuous: "What is this dog wearing?"

💭 Things to Remember

Not only people wear costumes — animals can wear them too!
Different animals wear different costume items: dogs wear wings, manes, masks, fins, and snouts.
The book ends with a fun question, inviting readers to guess what the last dog is wearing.
