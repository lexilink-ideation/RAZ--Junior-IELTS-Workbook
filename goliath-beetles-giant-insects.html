Story

Read the story. Target words are highlighted. 多读几次！
🪲 A huge Goliath beetle next to a human hand and a larva
Page 4
Goliath beetles are the giants of the insect world. Adult Goliaths can be bigger than a songbird. A young Goliath beetle is called a larva. A larva can be even bigger than an adult beetle!
🔍 Rhinoceros, Hercules and Goliath beetles with their body parts labeled
Page 5–6
All beetles have two sets of wings. The large wings in the back are for flying. The front wings are smaller and harder. They protect the flying wings. All insects have wings, legs with joints, three main body parts, and an outside skeleton.
🦓 Goliath beetles with black stripes and plain brown ones
Page 7–8
Most Goliath beetles have black stripes. Some beetles are plain brown. No two beetles are the same. People once called Goliath beetles "zebra beetles" because of their stripes. Beetles have mouths that help them chew. Some insects, like butterflies, have mouths like straws. Other insects have mouths like needles.
🌍 A map of Africa, and a male Goliath beetle with horns
Page 9–10
Goliath beetles live in forests and grassy parts of Africa. They are only seen during the rainy time of year. That's when there is plenty of fruit for them to eat. Male Goliath beetles fight other males over feeding places. Male beetles may get mates by having good feeding places. Female Goliath beetles do not have horns. Only males have horns.
🥚 The Goliath beetle life cycle: eggs, larvae and an adult
Page 11–12
Female Goliath beetles put their eggs in a deep hole. Larvae hatch about two weeks later. The larvae eat dead plants in the soil and grow fast. Larvae can be as long as your hand. They are as heavy as five mice! As soon as the rain ends, the larvae go deep into the ground. They cover themselves with a thin coat of sand.
🌳 A Goliath beetle climbs a tree in a rainforest
Page 13–15
Larvae change into adult beetles inside the coat of sand. Adults come out when the rain begins again. In zoos, Goliath beetles live to be about eight months old. In the wild, Goliath beetles must find food and escape enemies. They may only live for a few months. People have been studying Goliath beetles for hundreds of years. They are rare and interesting to see. Goliath beetles are amazing insects!
