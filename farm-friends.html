Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🌾farm(n.)
农场▾
Simple meaning
a place where people grow food and raise animals
Example
"Would you like to go to my grandpa's farm?" asks Jupe.
👴grandpa(n.)
爷爷 / 外公▾
Simple meaning
your father's or mother's father
Example
"Hello!" says Grandpa. "You are just in time to help me feed the animals."
🍪cookies(n.)
饼干▾
Simple meaning
sweet, baked treats you eat as a snack
Example
"Cookies will help me like the farm," says Bonk.
🚌bus(n.)
公共汽车▾
Simple meaning
a big vehicle that carries many people
Example
Soon the bus comes. "Ready?" says Jupe.
🐴horses(n.)
马▾
Simple meaning
large animals that people can ride
Example
He sees horses. He sees hills and fences.
🌿hay(n.)
干草▾
Simple meaning
dried grass that farm animals eat
Example
Bonk gives hay to the cows.
🏚️barn(n.)
谷仓▾
Simple meaning
a big building on a farm where animals or hay are kept
Example
Then Jupe and Bonk play near the barn.
🛞swing(v./n.)
摇摆；秋千▾
Simple meaning
to move back and forth in the air
Example
They swing on the tire swing.
😩growls(v.)
（肚子）咕咕叫▾
Simple meaning
makes a low rumbling sound (like a hungry tummy)
Example
Bonk's tummy growls. He smells cookies!
🍽️feed(v.)
喂养▾
Simple meaning
to give food to a person or animal
Example
"You are just in time to help me feed the animals."

Story

Read the story. Target words are highlighted. 多读几次！
👹🧒 Jupe asks Bonk the little monster to visit her grandpa's farm. Bonk worries there is no mall or skatepark there.
Page 3-4
"Would you like to go to my grandpa's farm?" asks Jupe. "Is there a mall by the farm?" Bonk asks. "No," says Jupe. "Is there a skatepark?" asks Bonk. "No," says Jupe. "But there are cows." "Oh," says Bonk. "There are pigs, too," says Jupe. "I don't think I like farms," says Bonk.
🍪🚌 Jupe tells Bonk about her grandma's cookies, and Bonk starts to feel better. Then the bus arrives to take them to the farm.
Page 5-6
Then Jupe tells Bonk about her grandma's cookies. "Cookies will help me like the farm," says Bonk. Soon the bus comes. "Ready?" says Jupe.
🐴🚌 Bonk looks out the bus window at horses, hills, and fences. Grandpa greets them warmly when they arrive at the farm.
Page 7-8
Bonk watches out the window of the bus. He sees horses. He sees hills and fences. Then Bonk sees a farm. "Hello!" says Grandpa. "You are just in time to help me feed the animals."
🐄🌿 Bonk and Jupe feed the farm animals, then play near the barn and swing on a tire swing. Bonk's tummy rumbles when he smells cookies.
Page 9-10
Bonk gives hay to the cows. Jupe gives corn to the pigs. The cats meow for their dinner, too. Then Jupe and Bonk play near the barn. They swing on the tire swing. Bonk's tummy growls. He smells cookies!
🐐🐔 Jupe guesses the goats or chickens, but Bonk grins — it's time to feed the "monsters," meaning himself and Jupe with grandma's cookies!
Page 11-12
"I think it's time to feed someone else," says Bonk. "The goats?" asks Jupe. "The chickens?" asks Jupe. "No," says Bonk. "The monsters!"

📐 Key Grammar

Yes/No questions with "Is there...?" — "Is there a mall by the farm?" asks Bonk.
Quoted speech with "says + name" — "Cookies will help me like the farm," says Bonk.
Simple present tense for narration — "He sees horses." / "Bonk gives hay to the cows."

💭 Things to Remember

Bonk is a little monster who visits a real farm with his friend Jupe.
Farm animals need people to feed them, like hay for cows and corn for pigs.
Bonk changes his mind about farms once he has fun and smells cookies.
