Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🌱crops(n.)
庄稼▾
Simple meaning
plants that farmers grow for food, like wheat or corn
Example
"Farmers need rain to grow their crops," the cloud said.
📦goods(n.)
货物▾
Simple meaning
things that are made or grown to be sold or moved
Example
"Ships need rain to move goods over the water," the cloud said.
🚧dams(n.)
水坝▾
Simple meaning
big walls built across rivers to hold back water
Example
"Dams need rain to make power," the cloud said.
⚡power(n.)
电力▾
Simple meaning
energy used to run lights, machines, and homes
Example
Dams need rain to make power.
🚒firefighters(n.)
消防员▾
Simple meaning
people whose job is to put out fires
Example
"Firefighters need rain to help put out forest fires," the cloud said.
🤔wondered(v.)
想知道；纳闷▾
Simple meaning
to think about something and want to know the answer
Example
"Who needs rain?" wondered Andy before he went to bed.
☁️cloud(n.)
云▾
Simple meaning
a white or gray shape in the sky made of tiny drops of water
Example
Andy dreamed about a cloud.
🌊rivers(n.)
河流▾
Simple meaning
long lines of water that flow across land
Example
"Fish need rain to keep rivers and lakes full," the cloud said.
😴dreamed(v.)
做梦▾
Simple meaning
had pictures and stories in your mind while sleeping
Example
Andy dreamed about a cloud.
🔥forest fires(n.)
森林大火▾
Simple meaning
big fires that burn trees in a forest
Example
Firefighters need rain to help put out forest fires.

Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.Rain kept Andy inside for three days in a row.
2.Andy dreamed about a talking sun.
3.The cloud said farmers need rain to grow their crops.
4.Firefighters need rain to help put out forest fires.
5.At the end of the story, Andy says he does not want any more rain.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.Why couldn't Andy play outside?
2.Who does Andy meet in his dream?
3.Why do fish need rain?
4.What do dams need rain to make?
5.What happens right after Andy wakes up?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.What are two things the cloud says need rain?

Hint: Look at pages 6-11 — farmers, fish, animals, firefighters, dams, and ships all need rain for different reasons.
2.How did Andy feel at the start of the story, and how did his feelings about rain change by the end?

Hint: At first Andy just wanted to play outside. After his dream, he tells his mom "we need rain, too!"
3.What do you think is the most important reason we need rain? Why?

Speaking

Practice speaking out loud! 大声说出来！

🟢 Level 1 — Simple Answers

🎤Do you like rainy days? Yes or no?
🎤Who does Andy meet in his dream?
🎤What comes out at the end of the story — rain or sun?

🟡 Level 2 — Short Sentence Answers

🎤Use the word "crops" to say why farmers need rain.
🎤Use the word "power" to explain what dams need rain for.
🎤Use the word "wondered" to describe what Andy did before bed.

🔵 Level 3 — Open-Ended Questions

🎤Why do you think the author chose a cloud to explain rain to Andy?
🎤Can you think of another reason rain is important that the cloud did not mention?
🎤How did Andy's opinion about rain change from the beginning to the end of the story? Why do you think that happened?
Useful phrases: ___ need(s) rain to ___. | "Who needs rain?" wondered Andy. | The cloud said... | That's great, but...

📐 Key Grammar

Past tense verbs: dreamed, wondered, laughed, called (add -ed)
"need + to + verb": Farmers need rain to grow their crops.
Quotation marks show exact speech: "Who needs rain?" laughed the cloud.

💭 Things to Remember

Rain helps farmers, fish, animals, firefighters, dams, and ships.
Andy learned that rain is important, even though he wanted sun.
A dream can teach a character (and the reader) something new.
