🧩 Sentence Patterns

Fog looks like ___.
Fog can form out of ___.
Fog always/sometimes forms ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Adverbs of Frequency: 'always' and 'sometimes' show how often fog forms in different places
Modal Verb 'can': shows something is possible — "Fog can form out of small water drops."
Prepositional Phrases: "near water," "near mountains," "near the ground" tell us where

💭 Things to Remember

Fog is made of tiny drops of water or ice close to the ground.
Fog forms near water, mountains, and wet land when the air is still.
Fog always makes it hard to see, so it can be a little spooky and even dangerous.
