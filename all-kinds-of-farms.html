🚜farm(n.)
农场▾
Simple meaning
a place where people grow plants and keep animals
Example

🐄cow(n.)
奶牛▾
Simple meaning
a big farm animal that gives us milk
Example
Milk comes from cows.
🥛milk(n.)
牛奶▾
Simple meaning
a white drink that comes from cows
Example
People drink milk.
🧈butter(n.)
黄油▾
Simple meaning
a soft yellow food made from milk
Example
People make cheese and butter from milk.
🐑sheep(n.)
绵羊▾
Simple meaning
a farm animal with soft white hair called wool
Example
Sheep live on farms.
🧶wool(n.)
羊毛▾
Simple meaning
the soft warm hair of a sheep, used to make clothes
Example
People make clothes from wool.
🐔chicken(n.)
鸡▾
Simple meaning
a farm bird that lays eggs
Example
Chickens live on farms.
🥚egg(n.)
鸡蛋▾
Simple meaning
a round food from a chicken that people cook and eat
Example
People eat eggs for breakfast.
🎋sugarcane(n.)
甘蔗▾
Simple meaning
a tall sweet plant that sugar comes from
Example
Sugarcane grows on farms.
🌱cotton(n.)
棉花▾
Simple meaning
a soft white plant that people use to make clothing
Example
Cotton grows on farms.

Speaking

Practice speaking out loud! 大声说出来！

🟢 Level 1 — Simple Answers

🎤Do cows live on farms? Yes or no?
🎤What comes from chickens — eggs or wool?
🎤Is sugar sweet?

🟡 Level 2 — Short Sentence Answers

🎤What comes from cows? Use ___ comes from ___.
🎤What grows on farms? Use ___ grows on farms.
🎤What do people make from wool? Use a full sentence.

🔵 Level 3 — Open-Ended Questions

🎤How do farms provide for us?
🎤Which farm would you like to visit — a dairy farm, a chicken farm, or a fruit farm? Why?
🎤What would we do if there were no farms?
Useful phrases: ___ live on farms. | ___ comes from ___. | ___ grows on farms. | People make ___ from ___.

Singular vs. plural verb: Sheep live / Cotton grows
make / use ... from ... : People make clothes from wool.

💭 Things to Remember

Animals like cows, sheep and chickens live on farms.
Plants like apples, sugarcane and cotton grow on farms.
Farms give us food to eat and materials for clothes.
