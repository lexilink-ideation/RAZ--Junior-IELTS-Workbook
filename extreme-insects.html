Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
⚡extreme(adj.)
极端的；非同寻常的▾
Simple meaning
much more than usual
Example
Let’s look at some of the extreme traits insects have.
🧬traits(n.)
特征；特点▾
Simple meaning
qualities that make living things different
Example
Insects have many traits that help them stay alive.
🛡️protect(v.)
保护▾
Simple meaning
to keep something from being hurt
Example
Extreme size and a hard cover protect it from its enemies.
👾enemies(n.)
敌人；天敌▾
Simple meaning
animals that want to hurt or eat you
Example
Insects have many enemies.
🐛caterpillar(n.)
毛毛虫▾
Simple meaning
the larva of a butterfly or moth
Example
A Sphinx moth caterpillar can puff up its spots.
☠️poison(n.)
毒；毒液▾
Simple meaning
a substance that can kill animals or plants
Example
It shoots out poison that turns their insides to goo.
⚔️weapons(n.)
武器▾
Simple meaning
things used to fight or hurt
Example
Some insects have extreme weapons to help them survive.
🕳️trap(v.)
诱捕；困住▾
Simple meaning
to catch something so it cannot get away
Example
An antlion larva traps and eats ants.
🦷jaws(n.)
颚；下巴▾
Simple meaning
the parts of the mouth that bite and hold
Example
It grabs an ant in its large, sharp jaws.
💨spray(n.)
喷雾；喷出的液体▾
Simple meaning
tiny drops of liquid that shoot out
Example
The bombardier beetle’s spray keeps enemies away.

Story

Read the story. Target words are highlighted. 多读几次！
⚠️ A tiny insect surrounded by dangers
Page 4
Insects are always in danger. They have many enemies. They are food for each other as well as other animals.
🔍 A magnifying glass over many different insects
Page 5
Insects have many traits that help them stay alive. These traits include size, looks, and weapons. Let’s look at some of the extreme traits insects have that help them.
🪲 A huge Goliath beetle next to a pen
Page 6
Extreme Size: Goliath beetles are one of the largest insects in the world. This beetle can grow as long as a pen. It can weigh as much as a banana. Extreme size and a hard cover protect it from its enemies.
🌿 A walking stick that looks just like a branch
Page 7
A walking stick’s size and looks help keep it from being eaten. This insect can grow longer than a new pencil. Its size frightens smaller insects away. Animals don’t eat it because they think it is just a big stick.
🗑️ A trash bug covered with leftovers
Page 8
Extreme Look-Alikes: Many insects look like other things. Their looks help them hide from animals that want to eat them. Trash bugs carry leftovers on their backs to hide from animals that might eat them.
🍃 A katydid that looks like a green leaf
Page 9
Some insects look like parts of plants. They can look like leaves, flowers, stems, or even thorns.
🐍 A Sphinx moth caterpillar puffing up like a cobra
Page 10
There are insects with large fake eyes to scare hungry animals away. When scared, this caterpillar can make itself look like a snake. A Sphinx moth caterpillar can puff up its spots so it looks like a cobra.
🐝 An ambush bug waiting on a flower
Page 11
Extreme Weapons: Some insects have extreme weapons to help them survive. Some of these insects use their weapons to get food. Others use their weapons to keep from becoming food. Ambush bugs have thick front legs to catch bees and flies.
🐜 An antlion larva waiting in a sand pit for ants
Page 12
An antlion larva traps and eats ants with its extreme jaws. This insect digs a pit in the sand. It traps ants in the pit. Then it grabs an ant in its large, sharp jaws. Yummy!
☠️ An assassin bug with a long sharp beak
Page 13
The assassin bug’s beak makes it deadly. This bug jumps on other insects. It stabs them with its sharp beak. It shoots out poison that turns their insides to goo. Then the bug sucks out the goo. Slurp!
💥 A bombardier beetle spraying hot liquid
Page 14
The bombardier beetle’s spray keeps enemies away. Watch out! This beetle sprays out boiling hot poison when touched. It stops animals from trying to eat it.
🛡️ Many insects safe in their own special ways
Page 15
Conclusion: Insects have many ways to protect themselves. Their size, color, and weapons can help them stay alive.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

Some insects ___. Others ___.
___ can grow as long as ___.
___ help them stay alive.

📐 Key Grammar

as + adjective + as: as long as a pen, as much as a banana.
to + verb shows purpose: to catch bees and flies, to scare animals away.
Comparative and superlative: longer than a pencil; one of the largest insects.

💭 Things to Remember

Insects face many enemies, so they need traits to survive.
Size, looks and weapons help insects protect themselves.
Some insects hide (look-alikes) and some fight back (weapons).
