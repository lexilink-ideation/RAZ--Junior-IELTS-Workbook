🧩 Sentence Patterns

Mix the ___ and ___ colors.
They turn into ___.
___ and ___ make ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Imperative (command) sentences: "Mix the red and blue colors." (verb first, no subject)
Present simple tense for facts: "They turn into purple."
Joining two nouns with "and": "the red and blue colors"

💭 Things to Remember

Red + blue = purple
Yellow + red = orange
Blue + yellow = green, and green + purple = brown
