🧩 Sentence Patterns

I am a ___.
A ___ helps the community.
When I grow up, I want to be a ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple Present with 'to be': "I am a doctor" tells what someone is right now
Indefinite Article 'a': used before a job name that starts with a consonant sound — "a nurse," "a pilot"
Noun + Noun Compounds: two words together make one job name — "police officer," "construction worker"

💭 Things to Remember

Community workers all have different jobs that help people.
Doctors, nurses, and vets take care of people and animals.
Firefighters, police officers, and construction workers keep our community safe and strong.
