Vocabulary

2️⃣double(v./adj.)
两倍 / 加倍▾
Simple meaning
to make something two times as many or as big; two times the amount
Example
The magic box will double anything you put inside!
🔍found(v.)
发现▾
Simple meaning
discovered or came upon something you were looking for (past tense of "find")
Example
Mia found a magic box with "Double It!" written on the side.
📦inside(prep./adv.)
里面▾
Simple meaning
in the inner part of something; within
Example
She looked inside the box and saw two books!
🤲put(v.)
放入▾
Simple meaning
to move something and place it in a position
Example
Mia put her shoes in the box to see what would happen.
👀looked(v.)
查看▾
Simple meaning
used your eyes to see something (past tense of "look")
Example
She looked inside the box and was very surprised!
🍬pieces(n.)
块 / 个▾
Simple meaning
separate individual items or parts of something
Example
Mia put seven pieces of candy in the box. Now there were fourteen!
💡idea(n.)
主意▾
Simple meaning
a thought or plan that comes into your mind
Example
Mia had a big idea — she wanted to climb inside the magic box!
⚠️trouble(n.)
麻烦▾
Simple meaning
a problem or difficult situation; something that causes worry
Example
"Double Mia would be double trouble!" said Mom with a laugh.
🧗climb(v.)
爬▾
Simple meaning
to go up or into something using your hands and feet
Example
Mia started to climb inside the box, but Mom said no!
📢cried(v.)
喊道▾
Simple meaning
said something in a loud, strong voice (past tense of "cry" — here it means shouted, not tears!)
Example
"No, Mia!" cried Mom when she saw Mia climbing into the box.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

📐 Key Grammar

Past Tense: found, put, looked, cried, laughed — actions that already happened
"Now there were ___": past tense to show an immediate result
Direct speech: "___!" said/asked/cried — quotation marks show someone speaking

💭 Things to Remember

The magic box doubles everything put inside — 1 becomes 2, 3 becomes 6!
"Double" means two times as many or as big.
"Double trouble" is a fun rhyme meaning twice as much trouble!
