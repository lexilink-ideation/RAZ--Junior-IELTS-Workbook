Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🏠home(n.)
家▾
Simple meaning
a place where a person or family lives
Example
People live in homes.
🏡house(n.)
房子▾
Simple meaning
a home that stands on its own, with its own roof and door
Example
A house has its own roof and front door.
🏢apartment(n.)
公寓▾
Simple meaning
a home that is one of several homes sharing one roof in a building
Example
Neighbors live on other floors in an apartment.
🛖cabin(n.)
小木屋▾
Simple meaning
a small, simple home, often made of logs, usually in nature
Example
A cabin can be made of logs.
🚐motor home(n.)
房车▾
Simple meaning
a home on wheels that can move from place to place
Example
A motor home can move from place to place.
🛥️houseboat(n.)
船屋▾
Simple meaning
a home that floats on the water
Example
A houseboat floats on water.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑neighbor(n.)
邻居▾
Simple meaning
a person (or thing) that lives near you
Example
In a cabin, trees and birds are your neighbors.
🌊float(v.)
漂浮▾
Simple meaning
to rest on top of water without sinking
Example
A houseboat can float on water.
🌟unique(adj.)
独特的▾
Simple meaning
special and different from all others
Example
Some special homes may be in a unique place.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

A ___ is another kind of home.
It has ___.
Homes can be ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense for facts: "A house has its own roof and front door."
"can / can't" for ability: "A motor home can move from place to place."
Comparatives with "or": "Homes can be big or small."

💭 Things to Remember

Houses stand on their own with their own roof and door.
Apartments share one roof, with neighbors living on different floors.
Houseboats float on water, and motor homes have wheels so they can move.
