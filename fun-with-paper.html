📐 Key Grammar

Modal "can" + base verb: You can fold paper.
Action verbs: change, cut, tear, roll, fold, glue, tape.
"can be" describes what is possible: Paper can be fun.

💭 Things to Remember

We use tools like scissors, glue, and tape.
