🧩 Sentence Patterns

We ___ on Thanksgiving.
We ___ (something) on Thanksgiving.
We visit family.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present: things we usually do. — We eat on Thanksgiving.
Subject "We": me and other people. — We drive on Thanksgiving.
Verb + object: some verbs need an object. — watch football, play games, visit family.

💭 Things to Remember

Thanksgiving is a fall family holiday.
Families drive to visit one another.
We cook, eat, watch football, play games, and rest.
