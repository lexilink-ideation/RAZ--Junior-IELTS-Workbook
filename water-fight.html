🎈water balloon(n.)
水球▾
Simple meaning

Example

🪣bucket(n.)
水桶▾
Simple meaning
a round container with a handle for carrying water
Example
William got soaked with a bucket.
🧽sponge(n.)
海绵▾
Simple meaning
a soft thing that holds water
Example
Pancho got soaked with a sponge.
🔫squirt gun(n.)
水枪▾
Simple meaning
a toy gun that shoots water
Example
Emma got soaked with a squirt gun.
🚿hose(n.)
水管▾
Simple meaning
a long tube that water comes out of
Example
Nora got soaked with a hose.
🍝noodle(n.)
泡沫游泳条▾
Simple meaning
a long, soft foam tube that kids play with in water
Example
Luis got soaked with a noodle.
🛁bath toy(n.)
洗澡玩具▾
Simple meaning
a toy you play with in the bath
Example
Jasmine got soaked with a bath toy.
👥everyone(pron.)
每个人；大家▾
Simple meaning
all the people
Example
Madison got soaked by everyone!
🤾water fight(n.)
水仗▾
Simple meaning
a game where people throw or spray water at each other
Example
A water fight is fun in summer.

by + person = who did it: soaked by everyone.
