Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🪽wings(n.)
翅膀；机翼▾
Simple meaning
the parts of a bird or plane that help it fly
Example
Some made wings out of wood.
🪂glider(n.)
滑翔机▾
Simple meaning
a light plane without an engine
Example
George Cayley built the first glider that carried a person.
⚙️engine(n.)
发动机▾
Simple meaning
a machine that makes things move
Example
Airplanes use engines to stay in the air.
🌀propeller(n.)
螺旋桨▾
Simple meaning
spinning blades that push a plane through the air
Example
Planes that fly low or slow often use propeller engines.
🚁helicopter(n.)
直升机▾
Simple meaning
an aircraft with big spinning blades on top
Example
Helicopters can take off straight up.
🎈blimp(n.)
飞艇▾
Simple meaning
a long balloon with engines that pilots steer
Example
Blimps are like long balloons.
🎈balloon(n.)
气球；热气球▾
Simple meaning
a big bag filled with hot air or gas that floats
Example
Hot-air balloons are pushed by the wind.
📦cargo(n.)
货物▾
Simple meaning
things carried by a plane, ship or truck
Example
Planes carry cargo all around the world.
🧳passengers(n.)
乘客▾
Simple meaning
people who ride in a vehicle
Example
This airplane carries hundreds of passengers.
👨‍✈️pilot(n.)
飞行员▾
Simple meaning
a person who flies a plane
Example
Blimps have engines and are steered by pilots.

🚑🚁 A helicopter ambulance landing on top of a building
