Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🍰dessert(n.)
甜点▾
Simple meaning
sweet food that you eat after a meal, like cake or ice cream
Example
The family all want the same dessert — cheesecake!
🤔problem(n.)
问题▾
Simple meaning
a difficult situation that needs to be fixed or solved
Example
They have a problem — there is only one piece of cake left!
✊grabs(v.)
抓/夺▾
Simple meaning
takes something very quickly with the hand
Example
Dad grabs the plate from the fridge before anyone else can.
🕊️soars(v.)
飞起▾
Simple meaning
flies up fast and high through the air
Example
The cake flies off the plate and soars through the air.
😋gobbles(v.)
狼吞虎咽▾
Simple meaning
eats something very quickly and greedily
Example
In seconds, Bruno gobbles up the cake.
🎉invited(v.)
被邀请▾
Simple meaning
asked to come along or join in something
Example
The family goes out for ice cream, but Bruno isn't invited!
💪tug(v.)
用力拉▾
Simple meaning
to pull something hard, usually back and forth
Example
Everyone grabs the plate. They pull and tug, back and forth.
❄️fridge(n.)
冰箱▾
Simple meaning
a cold machine in the kitchen that keeps food fresh
Example
One last piece of cake sits in the fridge.
🧁cheesecake(n.)
芝士蛋糕▾
Simple meaning
a sweet dessert made with cream cheese on a biscuit base
Example
The family all want cheesecake — but there is only one piece left!
💡idea(n.)
主意/点子▾
Simple meaning
a thought or plan that comes into your mind
Example
Then Mia has an idea — the family can go out for ice cream instead!

Story

Read the story. Target words are highlighted. 多读几次！
🍰 A family standing in the kitchen, looking at one piece of cheesecake sitting on a plate inside the fridge
Pages 3–4
The family all want the same dessert. They all want cheesecake. They have a problem, though. One last piece of cake sits in the fridge. Who should get to eat it?
😠 Dad reaching into the fridge and taking the plate of cake — Mom looking surprised and unhappy
Pages 5–6
Dad says he should have the cake. He grabs the plate from the fridge. Mom says she should have the cake. She grabs the plate from Dad.
🙅 Mia pulling the plate away from Mom — Papa reaching out to grab it from Mia
Pages 7–8
Mia says she should have the cake. She grabs the plate from Mom. Papa says he should have the cake. He grabs the plate from Mia.
👐 The whole family — Dad, Mom, Mia, Papa, and Nana — all grabbing the plate at the same time and pulling
Pages 9–10
Nana says she should have the cake. She grabs the plate from Papa. Everyone grabs the plate at the same time. They pull and tug, back and forth.
🐶 The cake flying through the air — Bruno the dog catching it in his mouth — the family looking shocked — then everyone walking to an ice cream shop without Bruno
Pages 11–12
The cake flies off the plate and soars through the air. Bruno catches it in his mouth. In seconds, he gobbles it up. Then, Mia has an idea. The family goes out for ice cream instead. Bruno isn't invited!
