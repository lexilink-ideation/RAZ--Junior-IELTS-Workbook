🧩 Sentence Patterns

This ___ has ___ ears.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple Present Tense (一般现在时): "has" describes a fact — "This fox has pointed ears."
Adjective + Noun Order (形容词+名词语序): the adjective comes before the noun — "big ears."
Yes/No Questions (一般疑问句): "Does this seal have ears?"

💭 Things to Remember

Different animals have different shapes and sizes of ears.
Ear shape can help us identify an animal.
Even animals with hard-to-see ears, like seals, still have ears.
