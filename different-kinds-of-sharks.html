Vocabulary

🦈shark(n.)
鲨鱼▾
Simple meaning
a big fish that lives in the ocean and has sharp teeth
Example
There are many different kinds of sharks.
🐋whale shark(n.)
鲸鲨▾
Simple meaning
a very big, gentle kind of shark
Example
A whale shark is very big.
📏big(adj.)
大的▾
Simple meaning
large in size
Example
A whale shark is very big.
⚪spots(n.)
斑点▾
Simple meaning
small round marks on an animal's skin
Example
A zebra shark has dark spots.
🟰stripes(n.)
条纹▾
Simple meaning
long thin lines of color
Example
A tiger shark has light stripes.
🐟fins(n.)
鳍▾
Simple meaning
the flat body parts a fish uses to swim
Example
An angel shark has wide fins.
💨fast(adj.)
快的▾
Simple meaning
moving very quickly
Example
A blue shark is very fast.
🔪sharp(adj.)
锋利的▾
Simple meaning
able to cut easily
Example
A great white shark has sharp teeth.
🦷teeth(n.)
牙齿▾
Simple meaning
the hard white parts in the mouth used for biting
Example
A great white shark has sharp teeth.
💧water(n.)
水▾
Simple meaning
the clear liquid found in oceans and rivers
Example

🧩 Sentence Patterns

A ___ shark is ___.
A ___ shark has ___.
All sharks live in ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple Present with 'is/has' to state facts — e.g. 'A whale shark is very big.'
Adjectives describe size and speed — e.g. big, fast.
Plural nouns for body parts — e.g. teeth, fins, spots, stripes.

💭 Things to Remember

Each shark looks different — some have spots, some have stripes.
