6月19日阅读笔记
📖 RAZ 分级读物 Level H ·《Statues in the Sand》
Lexile 460L  字数 208 words

阅读主题：
本书介绍了"沙雕师"这一特殊职业——他们是专业艺术家，能用沙子创作出令人惊叹的巨型雕像，主题涵盖真实人物、动物甚至整座城市。书中详细介绍了沙雕的制作步骤，并提到沙雕师会参加世界各地的沙雕比赛。

词汇（共8个）：
amazing 令人惊叹的 adj.
sand castles 沙堡 n.
sculptors 雕塑家 n.
carve 雕刻 v.
statues 雕像 n.
contests 比赛 n.
paid 获得报酬 v.（过去式）
giant 巨大的 adj.

短语：
get paid to do something 因做某事而获得报酬
sand sculptors 沙雕师
make sand statues 制作沙雕
pack the sand down 把沙子压实
carve the sand 雕刻沙子
go to contests around the world 参加世界各地的比赛

语法重点：
• 步骤顺序词（First / Next / Finally）：描述做事的先后步骤 — 书中例句：First, sand sculptors need a big pile of sand. Next, they jump on the wet sand. Finally, the sculptors begin to carve the sand.
• can + 动词原形（表示能力）：说明沙雕师能做到的事 — 书中例句：Sand sculptors can carve sand to look like real people.
• 不定式 to + 动词（表示目的）：说明某个行为的目的 — 书中例句：They are artists who use sand to make interesting statues.

重点句：
Sand sculptors use tools and their hands to carve the sand.
沙雕师用工具和双手来雕刻沙子。
句子结构：
Sand sculptors → 主语：沙雕师们
use tools and their hands → 谓语 + 并列宾语：用工具和双手
to carve the sand → 不定式短语，表示目的："为了雕刻沙子"

重点句2：
They are artists who use sand to make interesting statues.
他们是用沙子制作有趣雕像的艺术家。
句子结构：
They are artists → 主句：他们是艺术家
who use sand to make interesting statues → 定语从句，修饰 artists：具体说明这些艺术家用沙子做什么

例句：
1. You may have seen sand castles at the beach.
2. Some people get paid to play in the sand.
3. It takes a lot of work to make giant sand statues.
4. Sand sculptors use tools and their hands to carve the sand.
5. Sand sculptors go to contests around the world.
