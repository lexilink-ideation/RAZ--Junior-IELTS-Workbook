Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🐧penguin(n.)
企鹅▾
Simple meaning
a black and white bird that swims but cannot fly
Example
The penguin jumped into the cold water to swim.
🐦bird(n.)
鸟▾
Simple meaning
an animal with feathers and two wings
Example
A penguin is a bird, but it does not fly.
❄️cold(adj.)
寒冷的▾
Simple meaning
having a very low temperature; not warm at all
Example
Some penguins live in very cold places with ice and snow.
☀️warm(adj.)
温暖的▾
Simple meaning
having a gentle, comfortable heat; not cold
Example
Some penguins live in warm places near the ocean.
⬛black(adj.)
黑色的▾
Simple meaning
the darkest color, like the night sky
Example
Some penguins are black and white, like a tuxedo.
⬜white(adj.)
白色的▾
Simple meaning
the lightest color, like milk or snow
Example
The penguin's belly is white.
🐣little(adj.)
小的▾
Simple meaning
small in size; not big
Example
Some penguins are little — they can fit in your hands!
🐘big(adj.)
大的▾
Simple meaning
large in size; not little
Example
The Emperor penguin is a very big penguin.
🕊️fly(v.)
飞翔▾
Simple meaning
to move through the air with wings
Example
Penguins cannot fly, but other birds can.
🏊swim(v.)
游泳▾
Simple meaning
to move through water using the body
Example
All penguins swim well in the ocean.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

All penguins are ___.
Some penguins are ___.
All penguins cannot ___.

📐 Key Grammar

"All" = every penguin: All penguins are birds.
"Some" = part of the group: Some penguins are big.
Simple present tense: Penguins swim well.

💭 Things to Remember

Penguins are birds, but they cannot fly.
All penguins can swim very well.
Penguins can be big or little, and live in cold or warm places.
