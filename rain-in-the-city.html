☁️cloud(n.)
云▾
Simple meaning
a white or gray shape in the sky made of tiny drops of water
Example
I see the clouds.
🌧️rain(n.)
雨▾
Simple meaning
water that falls from clouds
Example
I see the rain.
⚡lightning(n.)
闪电▾
Simple meaning
a bright flash of light in the sky during a storm
Example
I see the lightning.
☂️umbrella(n.)
雨伞▾
Simple meaning
something you hold over your head to stay dry
Example
I see the umbrellas.
💧puddle(n.)
水坑▾
Simple meaning
a small pool of water on the ground
Example
I see the puddle.
🕳️drain(n.)
排水沟▾
Simple meaning
a pipe or hole that takes water away
Example
I see the drain.
🌈rainbow(n.)
彩虹▾
Simple meaning
a colorful arc of light in the sky after rain
Example
I see the rainbow.
☀️sun(n.)
太阳▾
Simple meaning
the bright star that gives us light and heat
Example
I see the Sun.
👀see(v.)
看见▾
Simple meaning

Example

Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

2.I see the clouds.
3.I see a dog in the rain.
4.I see the umbrellas.
5.The sun comes out after the rain.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.What do people use to stay dry in the rain?
2.What do you see first, before it rains?
3.What flashes brightly in the sky during a storm?
4.Where does the rainwater go on the street?
5.What appears in the sky after the rain stops?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.What do you see in the sky before it rains?

Hint: Look at page 3 — think about gray shapes in the sky.

Definite article "the": "I see the rain."

💭 Things to Remember

The weather changes during a storm: clouds, rain, lightning.
People use umbrellas to stay dry.
After rain, we may see a rainbow and the sun.
