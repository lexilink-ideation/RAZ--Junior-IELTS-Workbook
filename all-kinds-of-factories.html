Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🏭factory(n.)
工厂▾
Simple meaning
a big building where people and machines make things
Example
Factories are big buildings where goods are made.
📦goods(n.)
商品；货物▾
Simple meaning
things that are made so people can buy and use them
Example
Some factories have robots make goods.
🏙️city(n.)
城市▾
Simple meaning
a big busy place with many buildings and people
Example
Some factories are in the city.
🌄country(n.)
乡村；农村▾
Simple meaning
the quiet land outside the city, with fields and farms
Example
Some factories are in the country.
⚙️machine(n.)
机器▾
Simple meaning
a tool with moving parts that helps people do work
Example
Some factories use huge machines.
🤖robot(n.)
机器人▾
Simple meaning
a machine that can move and do a job by itself
Example
Some factories have robots make goods.
📄paper(n.)
纸▾
Simple meaning
a thin flat sheet we write or draw on, made from wood
Example
This factory turns wood into paper.
🌱cotton(n.)
棉花▾
Simple meaning
a soft white plant that people use to make cloth
Example
This factory turns cotton into cloth.
🧵cloth(n.)
布；布料▾
Simple meaning
soft flat material that people use to make clothes
Example
People use cloth to make clothes.
🪟glass(n.)
玻璃▾
Simple meaning
a hard clear material that you can see through
Example
People use glass in windows.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present for facts: Factories are big buildings.
Passive voice: Goods are made in factories.
turn A into B: This factory turns sand into glass.

💭 Things to Remember

A factory is a big building where goods are made.
Factories can be in the city or in the country.
Robots, people, or both can make goods.
