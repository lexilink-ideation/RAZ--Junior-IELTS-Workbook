🌊ocean(n.)

Example

🍽️hungry(adj.)

🙈hide(v.)

📐 Key Grammar

Past tense in a legend: had, slept, heard, found, left.
Would + verb for things that happened again and again: Nian would climb out of the ocean.
Adjectives describe nouns: sharp horn, huge mouth, bright red.

💭 Things to Remember

Nian is a dragon beast from a Chinese legend.
People used red, noise and firecrackers to keep Nian away.
This legend explains why people celebrate Chinese New Year’s Eve this way.
