Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🐞insect(n.)
昆虫▾
Simple meaning
a small animal with six legs, like a wasp or a butterfly
Example
Insects have many kinds of wings.
🪽wing(n.)
翅膀▾
Simple meaning
the body part an insect uses to fly
Example
Insects use their wings to fly.
🕊️fly(v.)
飞▾
Simple meaning
to move through the air
Example
Insects use their wings to fly.
🐝wasp(n.)
黄蜂▾
Simple meaning
a flying insect that can sting
Example
This wasp has blue wings.
🟤spotted(adj.)
有斑点的▾
Simple meaning
covered with small round marks
Example
This moth has spotted wings.
🦓striped(adj.)
有条纹的▾
Simple meaning
having lines of color
Example
This dragonfly has striped wings.
🔍clear(adj.)
透明的▾
Simple meaning
easy to see through, like glass
Example
This ant has clear wings.
🌈colorful(adj.)
色彩鲜艳的▾
Simple meaning
having many bright colors
Example
This butterfly has colorful wings.
🛡️cover(n.)
覆盖物▾
Simple meaning
a hard part that protects something underneath it
Example
Some insects have wing covers.
🦋butterfly(n.)
蝴蝶▾
Simple meaning
a flying insect with big, colorful wings
Example
This butterfly has colorful wings.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense for facts: "Insects use their wings to fly."
"This" + singular noun: "This wasp has blue wings."
Adjective before noun: "spotted wings," "clear wings," "colorful wings."
