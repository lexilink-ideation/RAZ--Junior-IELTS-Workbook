Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🤝caretaker(n.)
照顾者▾
Simple meaning
a person who takes care of something or someone
Example
This farmer is a caretaker.
💚take care of(v.)
照顾▾
Simple meaning
to look after someone or something and keep it safe or well
Example
She takes care of farms.
👩‍🌾farmer(n.)
农民▾
Simple meaning
a person who grows crops or raises animals on a farm
Example
This farmer is a caretaker. She takes care of farms.
🧑‍🍼babysitter(n.)
保姆▾
Simple meaning
a person who looks after children for a short time
Example
This babysitter is a caretaker. He takes care of children.
🚧road worker(n.)
道路工人▾
Simple meaning
a person whose job is to build and fix roads
Example
This road worker is a caretaker. She takes care of the roads.
🐾vet(n.)
兽医▾
Simple meaning
a doctor who takes care of sick or hurt animals
Example
This vet is a caretaker. He takes care of animals.
🩺doctor(n.)
医生▾
Simple meaning
a person whose job is to take care of sick people
Example
This doctor is a caretaker. She takes care of people.
🦷dentist(n.)
牙医▾
Simple meaning
a doctor who takes care of people's teeth
Example
This dentist is a caretaker. He takes care of teeth.
🚒firefighter(n.)
消防员▾
Simple meaning
a person whose job is to put out fires and keep people safe
Example
These firefighters and teachers are caretakers.
👩‍🏫teacher(n.)
老师▾
Simple meaning
a person whose job is to teach students in school
Example
Teachers are caretakers. They take care of people, too.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense for facts — add -s: "She takes care of farms."
Subject pronouns he/she — "He takes care of children."
Plural nouns with -s — "These firefighters and teachers are caretakers."

💭 Things to Remember

A caretaker is someone who takes care of something.
There are many kinds of caretakers, like farmers, vets, and teachers.
Every caretaker helps take care of something important in our community.
