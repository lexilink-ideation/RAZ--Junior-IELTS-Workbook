Vocabulary

🥞breakfast(n.)
早餐▾
Simple meaning
the first meal of the day
Example
Our family is going out for breakfast.
🪑table(n.)
餐桌▾
Simple meaning
a piece of furniture where people sit to eat
Example
We find our table.
📋menu(n.)
菜单▾
Simple meaning
a list of food you can order
Example
We read our menus.
📝order(v.)
点（餐）▾
Simple meaning
to ask for food in a restaurant
Example
We order our breakfast.
🥤drink(n.)
饮料▾
Simple meaning
something you drink, like juice or milk
Example
We get our drinks.
🍽️food(n.)
食物▾
Simple meaning
things that people eat
Example
We eat our food.
🧾check(n.)
账单▾
Simple meaning
the paper that shows how much you must pay
Example
We get our check.
💵bill(n.)
账单；费用▾
Simple meaning
the money you owe for food or things
Example
We pay our bill.
🧑‍🍳server(n.)
服务员▾
Simple meaning
a person who brings food to your table
Example
We thank our server.
👨‍👩‍👧family(n.)
家庭；家人▾
Simple meaning
a group of people who are related, like parents and children
Example
Our family loves going out for breakfast.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

We ___ our ___.
Our family is going out for ___.
We go out to eat.

📐 Key Grammar

Possessive adjective "our": "our table", "our food"
Present continuous: "Our family is going out for breakfast."
Simple present with we: "We read our menus."

💭 Things to Remember

Going out to eat has steps: find a table, read, order, eat, pay, thank.
A server brings your food and the check.
It is polite to thank the server.
