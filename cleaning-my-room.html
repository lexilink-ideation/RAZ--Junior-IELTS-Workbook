Vocabulary

🧹clean(v.)
打扫▾
Simple meaning
to make a place tidy and free of mess
Example
Mom says I have to clean my room.
🚪room(n.)
房间▾
Simple meaning
a space inside a house with walls
Example
I have to clean my room.
🗂️sort(v.)
分类▾
Simple meaning
to put things into groups by type
Example
Can you help me sort my clothes?
👕clothes(n.)
衣服▾
Simple meaning
things people wear, like shirts and socks
Example
Can you help me sort my clothes?
🧦socks(n.)
袜子▾
Simple meaning
soft coverings worn on the feet, inside shoes
Example
I need to find all my red socks.
👚shirts(n.)
衬衫▾
Simple meaning
clothes worn on the top part of the body
Example
I need to find all my green shirts.
🗃️pile(n.)
一堆▾
Simple meaning
a group of things stacked together
Example
I found four red socks and put them in a pile.
🔍find(v.)
找到▾
Simple meaning
to discover something you are looking for
Example
Can you help me find them?
🤝help(v.)
帮助▾
Simple meaning
to do something useful for someone
Example
Can you help me sort my clothes?
🙏thank(v.)
感谢▾
Simple meaning
to tell someone you are grateful
Example
Thank you for your help!

🧩 Sentence Patterns

Can you help me find my ___?
I found ___ and put them in a pile.
I need to find all my ___ ___.

📐 Key Grammar

"Have to" for obligation: "I have to clean my room."
Question form with "Can you...?": "Can you help me find them?"
Past tense "found": "I found four red socks."

💭 Things to Remember

The boy sorts his clothes by type and color.
He finds socks (red, blue) and shirts (green, yellow).
Sorting clothes into piles helps clean the room.
