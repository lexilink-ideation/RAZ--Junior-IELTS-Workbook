Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🐉dragonfly(n.)
蜻蜓▾
Simple meaning
a fast-flying insect with two pairs of wings and a long thin body that lives near water
Example
The dragonfly flew quickly over the pond, its wings shining in the sun.
🐛insect(n.)
昆虫▾
Simple meaning
a small creature with six legs and usually wings, such as a bee, butterfly, or dragonfly
Example
A dragonfly is an insect — it has six legs and a body with three parts.
🪁wings(n.)
翅膀▾
Simple meaning
flat body parts used for flying; dragonflies have two pairs (four wings total)
Example
Dragonflies have four wings and can fly in any direction.
🌬️breathe(v.)
呼吸▾
Simple meaning
to take air in and out of the body
Example
Young dragonflies can breathe underwater through special gills.
🧭direction(n.)
方向▾
Simple meaning
the way something is moving or pointing — forward, backward, up, down, etc.
Example
Dragonflies can change direction quickly while flying.
🏹hunt(v.)
猎食▾
Simple meaning
to chase and catch animals for food
Example
Dragonflies hunt small flying insects like mosquitoes.
☁️swarm(n.)
群▾
Simple meaning
a large group of insects flying or moving together
Example
A swarm of dragonflies flew over the lake in the summer evening.
🦋adult(n./adj.)
成年的▾
Simple meaning
fully grown; a dragonfly becomes an adult when it leaves the water and grows wings
Example
An adult dragonfly lives on land and can fly very fast.
👟pairs(n.)
对▾
Simple meaning
groups of two matching things; two pairs means four things in total
Example
Dragonflies have two pairs of wings — two on each side.
👁️sight(n.)
视力▾
Simple meaning
the ability to see; dragonflies have amazing sight because of their huge eyes
Example
Dragonflies have excellent sight and can see in almost every direction.

Story

Read the story. Target words are highlighted. 多读几次！
🐉 A bright green dragonfly hovering over the surface of a still pond
Pages 3–4 · Amazing Dragonflies
Dragonflies are amazing insects! They fly quickly through the air over ponds and rivers. Their long, thin bodies can be many different colors — green, blue, red, or brown. Some have stripes and can change color as they grow older.
🪁 Close-up of a dragonfly showing two pairs of wings spread wide and huge compound eyes
Pages 5–6 · What Are Dragonflies?
A dragonfly has a large head with enormous eyes. Its body is long and skinny. It has two pairs of wings — four wings in total. Their wings are thin and clear and catch the sunlight beautifully. They are some of the most colorful insects in the world.
💧 Young dragonfly nymphs underwater among water plants; they breathe underwater
Pages 7–8 · Young Dragonflies
Young dragonflies live in water. A mother dragonfly lays eggs in or near water. After the eggs hatch, young dragonflies called nymphs live underwater. They can breathe underwater through special body parts. They are usually green or brown so they blend in with plants — this is called camouflage. When they are ready to become adults, they crawl out of the water and grow their wings.
👁️ A huge close-up dragonfly eye — 30,000 tiny parts visible inside the large round eye
Pages 9–10 · Super Sight
Dragonflies have the largest eyes of any insect! Each eye is made of about 30,000 tiny parts. This gives them amazing sight — they can see in almost every direction at the same time, including behind them!
✈️ A dragonfly flying backward between pond plants; another flying upside down
Pages 11–12 · Amazing Flight
Dragonflies are the fastest flying insects in the world! They can move their four wings up and down separately, which lets them change direction very quickly. They can fly forward, backward, and even upside down!
🏹 A dragonfly swooping on a mosquito from behind and eating it mid-air
Pages 13–14 · Amazing Hunters
Dragonflies are excellent hunters. They hunt small flying insects such as mosquitoes. They approach their prey from behind or below, and they can catch and eat their food right in the air! Because they eat mosquitoes, dragonflies are very helpful to humans.
🐸 A frog eating a dragonfly; a large swarm of dragonflies flying together over water
Pages 15–16 · Predators and Swarms
Not all dragonflies are safe. Birds, lizards, frogs, and spiders all eat dragonflies. Amazingly, dragonflies sometimes eat each other too! When many dragonflies fly together in a large group, this is called a swarm. There are about 3,000 kinds of dragonfly found worldwide. The first dragonfly park in the world opened in Japan in 1985.
