Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🥧pie(n.)
派；馅饼▾
Simple meaning
a baked food with fruit inside
Example
Six cups of fruit will make a pie.
🧺picked(v.)
采摘（pick 的过去式）▾
Simple meaning
took fruit from a plant
Example
We picked eight cups of strawberries.
🥛cups(n.)
杯（cup 的复数）▾
Simple meaning
small containers used to measure food
Example
Three cups made it home.
✅enough(adj.)
足够的▾
Simple meaning
as much as you need
Example
Do we have enough?
🙌plenty(n.)
大量；充足▾
Simple meaning
a lot; more than enough
Example
Plenty for a pie!
🍓strawberries(n.)
草莓▾

Example

🫐blueberries(n.)

🍇blackberries(n.)

Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.Six cups of fruit will make a pie.
2.The raccoons picked eight cups of strawberries.
3.Five cups of berries made it home the first time.
4.The raccoons picked one pumpkin.
5.The raccoons did not make a pie.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.How many cups of fruit make a pie?
2.How many cups of blueberries did they pick?
3.What did they pick last?
4.How many cups made it home after the strawberries?
5.What lesson did the raccoons learn?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.What fruit did the raccoons pick? Name two.

Hint: Look at pages 4, 6 and 8.
2.Why did not enough fruit make it home?

Hint: Think: What did the raccoons do on the way home?
3.What is your favorite kind of pie?

📐 Key Grammar

Past tense: pick → picked (add -ed).
How many + plural noun: How many cups?
Plural nouns: cup → cups, strawberry → strawberries.
