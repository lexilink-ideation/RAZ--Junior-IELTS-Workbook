Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

2.Zots is from outer space.
3.Abby has many eyes.
4.Zots can have many legs.
5.Abby and Zots stop being friends.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.Where is Zots from?
2.How many eyes does Abby have?
3.How many legs can Zots have?
4.Abby and Zots are best ___.
