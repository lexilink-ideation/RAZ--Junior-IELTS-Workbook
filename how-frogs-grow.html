📐 Key Grammar

Simple Present（一般现在时）— facts that are always true: A frog lays eggs in water.
Third-person singular -s — one frog lays, but many tadpoles grow: The tadpoles grow back legs.
Plural nouns with -s — egg → eggs, leg → legs, tail → tails: The tadpoles have long tails.

💭 Things to Remember

A frog lays its eggs in water, and tadpoles come out of the eggs.
Tadpoles grow back legs first, then front legs.
The tail gets smaller and smaller, and the froglet becomes a frog.
