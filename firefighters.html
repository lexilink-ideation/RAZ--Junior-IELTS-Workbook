Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
👨‍🚒firefighters(n.)
消防员▾
Simple meaning
people whose job is putting out fires and saving lives
Example
Firefighters put out fires and save lives.
⛽fuel(n.)
燃料▾
Simple meaning
anything that is burned to produce heat or power
Example
Wood and oil are two kinds of fuel.
⚙️machines(n.)
机器▾
Simple meaning
things that use energy to help a person do work
Example
Fire trucks are important firefighting machines.
🚰pump(n.)
泵；抽水机▾
Simple meaning
a machine that moves liquid or gas
Example
A fire engine has a pump to spray water.
💦spray(v.)
喷洒▾
Simple meaning
to sprinkle or force out liquid in a mist or many small drops
Example
They use hoses to spray water or foam on fires.
🎓training(n.)
培训；训练▾
Simple meaning
the teaching of a skill or way to act
Example
Firefighters go to school for training.
🚿hose(n.)
水管；软管▾
Simple meaning
a long tube that carries water to spray on a fire
Example
Firefighters hold onto a powerful hose.
🌬️oxygen(n.)
氧气▾
Simple meaning
a gas in the air that fire needs in order to burn
Example
Oxygen is a gas in the air.
🆘rescue(n.)
救援▾
Simple meaning
the act of saving someone from danger
Example
Rescue trucks carry tools to help trapped people.
🧯extinguisher(n.)
灭火器▾
Simple meaning
a tool used to put out a small fire
Example
Fire extinguishers can put out small fires.

🚒 New York City firefighters leaving the fire station; firefighters teaching children about fire safety in a fun way
Page 4
Have you ever heard a fire truck racing to a fire? Firefighters put out fires and save lives. They help keep fires from starting. They teach people about fire safety.
🔥 Firefighters battling a large fire in New York; a "Do You Know?" box about fire and Earth
Page 5
Fires need air to burn. They also need fuel. Wood and oil are two kinds of fuel. The more fuel, the hotter the fire will be. But even a candle flame burns very hot. Do You Know? Earth is the only planet in our solar system where fire can burn because it has enough oxygen. Oxygen is a gas in the air.
🏢 Firefighters ready to put out many kinds of fires, from houses to forests
Page 6
Firefighters put out fires in houses and tall buildings. They also fight forest fires. They must be ready to put out many kinds of fires.
🚿 A diagram of firefighting tools: smoke mask, air tank, hose, fire extinguisher, and ax
Page 7
Firefighters use many different tools. They use hoses to spray water or foam on fires. They break down doors with axes. They use ladders to reach high places.
🧯 Firefighters holding a powerful hose in Seychelles, climbing a ladder in India, and using axes to fight a fire in the United Kingdom
Page 8
Smoke masks protect firefighters from smoke. Fans move smoke from rooms. Fire extinguishers can put out small fires.
🚒 Firefighters using a ladder truck to reach a fire high above the ground
Page 9
Fire trucks are important firefighting machines. A fire engine has a pump to spray water.
💧 A powerful pump spraying water onto a fire from above
Page 10
Ladder trucks carry ladders. Tanker trucks carry water. Rescue trucks carry tools to help trapped people.
🚤 A fireboat spraying water onto a fire in Germany; a plane dropping water to help fight a forest fire
Page 11
Other kinds of machines fight fires, too. Fireboats fight fires from the water. Helicopters drop water on fires. Planes help put out forest fires, too.
💪 A firefighter carrying heavy equipment, showing firefighters must be strong to do their jobs
Page 12
Firefighters must be at least eighteen years old. They must be strong and in good shape. They must also pass a written test.
🎓 Firefighters practicing at a fire school and learning to treat hurt people
Page 13
Firefighters go to school for training. They learn how to fight different kinds of fires. They also learn how to treat hurt people.
🔥 Firefighters learning how to save people's lives; fire can be useful, like for roasting marshmallows, but can also be very dangerous
Page 14
Fire is useful, of course. People have used it to cook and stay warm for many thousands of years. At the same time, fire can be very dangerous. Fires take many lives and destroy forests.
🚒 Firefighters risking their own lives to save the lives of others
Page 15
Fires also destroy thousands of buildings every year. Long ago, fires burned down entire cities. Today, firefighters put out fires and keep us safe.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

Firefighters use ___ to ___.
They must be ___ years old.
___ trucks carry ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Modal verb "must" for necessity — "Firefighters must be at least eighteen years old."
Infinitive of purpose "to + verb" — "They use hoses to spray water."
Comparative pattern "the more..., the more..." — "The more fuel, the hotter the fire will be."

💭 Things to Remember

Fire needs air (oxygen) and fuel to burn.
Firefighters use many tools and machines, like hoses, pumps, and ladder trucks.
Firefighters go through training to learn how to fight fires and help people.
