Vocabulary

🐾pet(n.)
宠物▾
Simple meaning
an animal that lives with you at home
Example
Jupe wants a pet.
🦖dinosaur(n.)
恐龙▾
Simple meaning
a huge animal that lived a very long time ago
Example
"Would you like a dinosaur?" says Lurk.
👽alien(n.)
外星人▾
Simple meaning
a creature that comes from outer space
Example
"What about an alien?" says Snag.
😱scary(adj.)
可怕的▾
Simple meaning
something that makes you feel afraid
Example
"A snake is too scary," says Jupe.
🎁present(n.)
礼物▾
Simple meaning
a gift that you give to someone
Example
Jupe opens a present from Lurk.
🎗️collar(n.)
项圈▾
Simple meaning
a band that goes around an animal's neck
Example

🤔guess(v.)
猜▾
Simple meaning
to try to give an answer without being sure
Example

☁️fluffy(adj.)

Simple meaning

Example

Example

🤲reaches(v.)

Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.Jupe wants a pet.
2.Lurk asks Jupe if she would like a snake.
3.The last present, from Bonk, is a mystery until Jupe opens it.
4.Jupe's new pet is a puppy.
5.Jupe's friends help take care of her new kitten.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.What does Lurk ask Jupe about?
2.Why doesn't Jupe want an alien?
3.What is in the present from Lurk?
4.What is Jupe's present in the last box?
5.Who asks, "Do you like your new pet?"

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.Why does Jupe say no to a dinosaur, a snake, an ant, and an alien?

Hint: Think about what Jupe says about each animal — too big, too scary, too little, or not a pet at all.
2.How does Jupe find out what her last present is?

Hint: Think about what Jupe does with the box — she listens to it, reaches into it, and looks inside it.
3.If you could get a new pet, what pet would you choose? Why?

🧩 Sentence Patterns

"Would you like a ___?" ... "It is too ___."
"___," says [Name].
"My present is ___."

📐 Key Grammar

Yes/No questions with "Would you like...?" — Example: "Would you like a dinosaur?" says Lurk.
Quotation marks for speech: "..." says [Name]. — Example: "My present is a kitten!" she says.
Simple present tense for actions — Example: She reaches into the box.
