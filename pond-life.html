💧pond(n.)
池塘▾
Simple meaning
a small area of still water where plants and animals live
Example
There are fish in the pond.
🌿plant(n.)
植物▾
Simple meaning
a living thing that grows in soil or water and is often green
Example
There are plants in the pond.
🐟fish(n.)
鱼▾
Simple meaning
an animal that lives and swims in water
Example
There are fish in the pond.
🐌snail(n.)
蜗牛▾
Simple meaning
a small, slow animal that carries a shell on its back
Example
There are snails in the pond.
🐞bug(n.)
虫子▾
Simple meaning
a small insect
Example
There are bugs in the pond.
🦆duck(n.)
鸭子▾
Simple meaning
a bird that swims on water and says "quack"
Example
There are ducks in the pond.
🐢turtle(n.)
乌龟▾
Simple meaning
an animal with a hard shell that lives in and near water
Example

🦫beaver(n.)
海狸▾
Simple meaning
a furry animal that builds dams in water with wood
Example
There are beavers in the pond.
👦boy(n.)
男孩▾
Simple meaning
a young male child
Example
There are boys in the pond.
🌱life(n.)
生物；生命▾
Simple meaning
all the living plants and animals in a place
Example

🧩 Sentence Patterns

There are ___ in the pond.
I can see a ___.
A ___ lives in the pond.

📐 Key Grammar

"There are + plural": say what exists (many). — There are fish in the pond.
Plural nouns: add -s for many (plant → plants); "fish" stays the same.
Preposition "in": something is inside. — in the pond.

💭 Things to Remember

A pond is home to many plants and animals.
Fish, snails, ducks, turtles, and beavers live in ponds.
Even a small pond is full of life.
