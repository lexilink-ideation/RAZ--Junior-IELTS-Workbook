Vocabulary

🐶dogs(n.)
狗▾
Simple meaning
animals that many people keep as pets; they bark and are friendly
Example
Allie and Ollie are dogs.
👭sisters(n.)
姐妹▾
Simple meaning
two or more girls or female animals who have the same parents
Example
Allie and Ollie are sisters.
🤔think(v.)
认为；思考▾
Simple meaning
to have an idea or opinion in your mind
Example
Allie and Ollie think they are people.
🧑‍🤝‍🧑people(n.)
人们▾
Simple meaning
human beings — men, women, and children
Example
Allie and Ollie sit like people.
🪑sit(v.)
坐▾
Simple meaning
to rest your body with your bottom on a chair or the floor
Example
Allie and Ollie sit like people.
🍽️eat(v.)
吃▾
Simple meaning
to put food in your mouth and swallow it
Example
Allie and Ollie eat like people.
🏃run(v.)
跑▾
Simple meaning
to move quickly using your legs
Example
Allie and Ollie run like people.
😴sleep(v.)
睡觉▾
Simple meaning
to rest with your eyes closed, usually at night
Example
Allie and Ollie sleep like people.
🐾chase(v.)
追逐▾
Simple meaning
to run after someone or something to try to catch it
Example
But Allie and Ollie chase cats just like dogs!
🐱cats(n.)
猫▾
Simple meaning
small furry animals that people often keep as pets; they say "meow"
Example
Allie and Ollie chase cats just like dogs!

Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.Allie and Ollie are dogs.
2.Allie and Ollie are brothers.
3.Allie and Ollie eat like people.
4.Allie and Ollie never run like people.
5.At the end of the story, Allie and Ollie chase cats just like dogs.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.What are Allie and Ollie?
2.How are Allie and Ollie related to each other?
3.What do Allie and Ollie think they are?
4.Which of these do Allie and Ollie do like people?
5.What do Allie and Ollie chase at the end of the story?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.What are three things Allie and Ollie do just like people?

Hint: Look at pages 6-9 — think about sit, eat, run, and sleep.
2.Why does the story use the word "But" before "Allie and Ollie chase cats"?

Hint: "But" shows a difference. Even though they act like people, at the end they do something dogs do.
3.Do you have a pet, or does someone you know have a pet, that acts like a person? What does it do?

🧩 Sentence Patterns

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense for habits — "Allie and Ollie eat like people."
Comparing with "like" — "sit like people," "run like people."
Contrast with "but" — "But Allie and Ollie chase cats just like dogs!"

💭 Things to Remember

Allie and Ollie are two dog sisters who act like people in many ways.
They sit, eat, run, and sleep just like people do.
Even so, they still chase cats just like real dogs.
