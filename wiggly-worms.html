🪱earthworm(n.)
蚯蚓▾
Simple meaning
a long soft animal without legs that lives in soil
Example
Earthworms are good for the soil.
💧slimy(adj.)
黏滑的▾
Simple meaning
wet and slippery
Example
Earthworms are slimy.
🌱soil(n.)
土壤▾
Simple meaning
the top layer of the ground where plants grow
Example
Earthworms make space in the soil.
🕳️tunnel(n.)
隧道；地道▾
Simple meaning
a long passage under the ground
Example
Earthworms build long tunnels underground.
🥚cocoon(n.)
茧；卵袋▾
Simple meaning
a covering that protects something
Example
The slime dries into a cocoon.
👩‍❤️‍👨mate(v.)
交配▾
Simple meaning
to come together to make babies
Example
After worms mate, a ball of slime forms.
💩waste(n.)
排泄物；废物▾
Simple meaning
solid matter passed out of an animal’s body
Example
Earthworm waste helps plants grow big and strong.
↔️stretch(v.)
伸展；拉长▾
Simple meaning
to make longer
Example
Each part can bend and stretch.
⬇️underground(adv.)
在地下▾
Simple meaning
below the surface of the earth
Example
Earthworms build long tunnels underground.
🌿plant(n.)
植物▾
Simple meaning
a living thing that grows in soil
Example
Earthworms eat dead leaves and plants.

Story

Read the story. Target words are highlighted. 多读几次！
🪱 A worm sticks its head out of its tunnel near some mushrooms
Page 4
Small Animals, Big Changes
Do you know about earthworms? These small animals change the soil in big ways.
🔍 A close-up of a worm’s ring-shaped body
Page 5
Earthworm Bodies
The body of an earthworm is made up of many parts. Each part is shaped like a ring. Each part can bend and stretch. Earthworms move by stretching and pulling their bodies.
💧 A slime trail on a log
Page 6
Slimy Worms!
Earthworms are slimy. The slime helps keep earthworms cool and wet. It also helps earthworms slide through soil. After worms mate, a ball of slime forms. The slime dries into a cocoon. Baby worms come out of the cocoon.
🕳️ A bird pulls a strong worm out of its hole
Page 7
Earthworm Behavior
Earthworms build long tunnels underground. The tunnels keep worms away from heat and sunlight. They also help earthworms hide from hungry animals.
🌙 A worm pulls a leaf into its hole at night
Page 8
Earthworms come out at night to look for food. Some people call them night crawlers. Earthworms eat dead leaves and plants.
👨‍🌾 Water and air reach plant roots through worm tunnels
Page 9
Earthworms Are Good for the Soil
Farmers love earthworms! Earthworms make space in the soil. The space helps plants get all what they need to grow. Earthworm waste helps plants grow big and strong.
🦔 A mole, a shrew, and a toad make meals of worms
Page 10
Wanted: Earthworms!
Many animals love to eat earthworms.
🌱 An earthworm pokes its head out of its hole
Page 12
Earthworms are small, but they have a big job. Earthworms help plants grow!

💭 Things to Remember
