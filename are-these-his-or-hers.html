🧩 Sentence Patterns

These ___ are his.
These ___ are hers.
Are these ___ his or hers?

📐 Key Grammar

Possessive pronouns "his" and "hers" show who owns something.
Plural nouns for clothing often end in -s (socks, shorts, jackets).
Yes/no questions with "Are these...?" ask about ownership.

💭 Things to Remember

The story is about sorting laundry between a boy and a girl.
Different clothes belong to different family members.
Some things, like jeans, can belong to both a boy and a girl.
