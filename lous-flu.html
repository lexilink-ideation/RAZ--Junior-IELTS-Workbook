Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🤧sneezing(v.)
打喷嚏▾
Simple meaning
when air bursts out of your nose and mouth suddenly, like "Achoo!"
Example
Lou is sneezing. Lou is wheezing.
😤wheezing(v.)
喘息，呼哧呼哧地喘气▾
Simple meaning
breathing with a rough, whistling sound because you do not feel well
Example
Lou is sneezing. Lou is wheezing.
🤒flu(n.)
流感▾
Simple meaning
a sickness that makes you cough, sneeze, and feel bad all over
Example
Lou is sick. He has the flu.
😷sick(adj.)
生病的▾
Simple meaning
not feeling well; your body does not feel good
Example
Stu is sick. He has the flu.
😩moaning(v.)
呻吟▾
Simple meaning
making a long, low sound because you feel bad
Example
Stu is moaning. Stu is groaning.
😖groaning(v.)
抱怨，呻吟▾
Simple meaning
making a deep, unhappy sound because something hurts or feels bad
Example
Stu is moaning. Stu is groaning.
😮‍💨cough(v.)
咳嗽▾
Simple meaning
to push air out of your throat suddenly, often with a loud sound
Example
He should not cough on sister Sue.
🤕aching(v.)
疼痛▾
Simple meaning
feeling a dull pain in your body
Example
Sue is aching. Sue is shaking.
🥶shaking(v.)
颤抖▾
Simple meaning
moving back and forth because you feel cold or sick
Example
Sue is aching. Sue is shaking.
🦠germs(n.)
细菌▾
Simple meaning
tiny living things you cannot see that can make you sick
Example
Wash your hands to stop the germs from spreading!

🧩 Sentence Patterns

___ is sick. He/She has the flu.
He/She should not sneeze/cough on ___.
Wash your hands. Don't sneeze on ___!

📐 Key Grammar

Present continuous (-ing) for symptoms happening now — "Lou is sneezing. Lou is wheezing."
Modal "should not" for advice — "He should not sneeze on brother Stu."
Pronouns he/she match the person's gender — "He has the flu." / "She has the flu."

💭 Things to Remember

Sneezing, coughing, and sharing without care can spread the flu to others.
Washing your hands can stop germs from spreading.
Good hygiene habits keep you and your family healthy.
