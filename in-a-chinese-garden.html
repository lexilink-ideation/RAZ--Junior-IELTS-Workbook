Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🚪gate(n.)
大门▾
Simple meaning
an opening in a wall or fence that you walk through to enter a place
Example
I enter a gate without doors.
💧water(n.)
水▾
Simple meaning
the clear liquid found in rivers, lakes, and ponds that people drink
Example
I see a lot of water.
🪨rock(n.)
岩石▾
Simple meaning
a big, hard piece of stone
Example
I see a huge rock.
🌳tree(n.)
树▾
Simple meaning
a tall plant with a trunk, branches, and leaves
Example
I see trees. They stand tall and give shade.
🪞mirror(n.)
镜子▾
Simple meaning
a smooth, shiny surface that shows your reflection
Example
It looks as smooth as a mirror.
🐘huge(adj.)
巨大的▾
Simple meaning
very, very big in size
Example
I see a huge rock.
☂️shade(n.)
树荫▾
Simple meaning
a cool, dark place out of the direct sunlight, often under a tree
Example
They stand tall and give shade.
🕊️peaceful(adj.)
宁静的▾
Simple meaning
calm and quiet, without noise or trouble
Example
The Chinese garden is beautiful and peaceful.
🌸beautiful(adj.)
美丽的▾
Simple meaning
very pretty or nice to look at
Example
The Chinese garden is beautiful and peaceful.
🧠remember(v.)
记住▾
Simple meaning
to keep something in your memory; to not forget it
Example
I'll remember it whenever I see water, rocks, and trees.

Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.The story takes place in a Chinese garden.
2.The narrator enters the garden through a gate with a big door.
3.The water in the garden looks as smooth as a mirror.
4.The huge rock in the garden moves and falls down easily.
5.The narrator says the Chinese garden is beautiful and peaceful.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.What does the water in the garden look like?
2.What does the narrator enter to go into the garden?
3.What does the huge rock do?
4.What do the tall trees give?
5.How does the narrator describe the Chinese garden at the end of the story?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.Why is the narrator glad about the huge rock?

Hint: The rock is so strong that it will not move or fall down. Think about how that makes the narrator feel about being strong, too.
2.What three things will the narrator remember about the Chinese garden?

Hint: Look at the last page of the story. Three garden features are named together in one sentence.
3.Would you like to visit a Chinese garden? Why or why not?

Speaking

🟢 Level 1 — Simple Answers

🟡 Level 2 — Short Sentence Answers

🎤Describe what the water in the garden looks like. Try to use the word "mirror".
🎤Why does the narrator feel glad about the huge rock? Try to use the words "huge" and "strong".
🎤What do the tall trees give people on a sunny day? Try to use the word "shade".

🧩 Sentence Patterns

I see a ___.
It looks as ___ as a ___.
I'm glad for ___ to ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present for description — "I see a huge rock."
Simile with as...as — "It looks as smooth as a mirror."
Future with will + time clause — "I'll remember it whenever I see water, rocks, and trees."

💭 Things to Remember

A Chinese garden has special features: a gate without doors, still water, huge rocks, and tall trees.
Chinese gardens are designed to feel beautiful and peaceful.
Each garden feature has its own Chinese character (word) that represents it.
