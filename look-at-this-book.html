Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🚀science fiction(n.)
科幻小说▾
Simple meaning
a story about science and the future
Example
When I read science fiction, I explore distant planets.
🪐planets(n.)
行星▾
Simple meaning
big round things in space, like Earth
Example
I explore distant planets.
🧭explore(v.)
探索▾
Simple meaning
to travel to learn about a new place
Example
I explore distant planets.
🧙fantasy(n.)
奇幻故事▾
Simple meaning
a story with magic and make-believe things
Example
When I read fantasy, I go to school to learn magic.
📖informational(adj.)
提供信息的▾
Simple meaning
giving facts about a topic
Example
When I read an informational book, I learn new facts.
👤biography(n.)
传记▾
Simple meaning
a book about a real person’s life
Example
When I read a biography, I learn about a person’s life.
🕵️mystery(n.)
悬疑故事▾
Simple meaning
a story with a puzzle to solve
Example
When I read a mystery, I play at being a detective.
🔍detective(n.)
侦探▾
Simple meaning
a person who finds clues to solve a mystery
Example
I play at being a detective.
✍️autobiography(n.)
自传▾
Simple meaning
a book people write about their own life
Example
When I read an autobiography, I hear someone tell his or her own story.
🌟experience(v.)
体验▾
Simple meaning
to feel or live through something
Example
Books let me experience new things.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

When I read ___, I ___.
A book can be a ___.
Books let me ___ new things.

📐 Key Grammar

When + simple present: “When I read fantasy, I go to school to learn magic.”
Can for possibility: “A book can be a time machine.”
Different book types: fantasy, mystery, biography, autobiography.

💭 Things to Remember

A book can be a time machine, a spaceship, or a magic doorway.
Each type of book takes the reader to a different place or gives a different experience.
Reading lets us experience new things without leaving the room.
