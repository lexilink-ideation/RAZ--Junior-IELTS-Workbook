🧩 Sentence Patterns

We see ___ by the path.
We see a new ___.
We see our ___ by the path.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense with "We see" describes what we notice right now — "We see a new forest path."
Adjective + noun word order: the describing word comes before the naming word — "big trees," "green moss," "brown birds."
The prepositional phrase "by the path" shows where something is — "We see small bugs by the path."

💭 Things to Remember
