🧩 Sentence Patterns

There are ___ (number) ___ (vegetable).
___ is greater than ___.
Which number is the greatest?

📐 Key Grammar

Plural noun + "are": "There are five carrots."
Comparative "greater than": "Four is greater than three."
Superlative "the greatest": "Which number is the greatest?"

💭 Things to Remember

Vegetables grow in a garden and we can count them.
We compare numbers to see which one is bigger, or greater.
The biggest number of all is called the greatest number.
