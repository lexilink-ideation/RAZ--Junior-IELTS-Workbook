🧩 Sentence Patterns

Carlos and his teacher ___.
They ___ together.
We ___ at school.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present verbs: talk, read, write, paint.
Plural subject + base verb: they read.
"and" joins two people: Carlos and his teacher.

💭 Things to Remember

Carlos and his teacher do many things together at school.
They read, write, paint, share, clean, and play.
School is a place to learn with your teacher.
