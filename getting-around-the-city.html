🧩 Sentence Patterns

Subject + go(es) to + place.
Subject + go(es) on/in + a vehicle.
We all go to + place.

📐 Key Grammar

-s ending: I/We go, but he/she goes. Example: "I go... She goes..."
"in" for cars/taxis, "on" for bikes/buses/subways/skateboards/ferry boats. Example: "She goes in a car." / "He goes on a ferry boat."
Possessive adjectives: my mom, my dad, my sister. Example: "My sister goes to school."

💭 Things to Remember

Different family members use different transportation to get around the city.
We say "in" a car or taxi, but "on" a bike, bus, subway, skateboard, or ferry boat.
Walking is a way almost anyone can use to get around, like the family at the end of the story.
