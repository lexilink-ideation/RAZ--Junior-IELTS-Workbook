Vocabulary

🧭directions(n.)
说明；指示▾
Simple meaning
words that tell you what to do
Example
I read the directions, and then I start the work.
👣follow(v.)
遵循；按照做▾
Simple meaning
to do what someone tells you to do
Example
I know how to follow these directions.
▶️start(v.)
开始▾
Simple meaning
to begin doing something
Example
I read the directions, and then I start the work.
🧠learn(v.)
学习▾
Simple meaning
to gain new knowledge or a skill
Example
Each day, my class learns about science, reading, and math.
📄work(n.)
作业；功课▾
Simple meaning
a task you do, like schoolwork
Example
We do work to show what we know.
📖passage(n.)
一段文字（课文）▾
Simple meaning
a short piece of writing that you read
Example
These directions say to read the passage.
⭕circle(v.)
圈出▾
Simple meaning
to draw a ring around something
Example
These directions say to circle the answer.
🎨color(v.)
涂色▾
Simple meaning
to fill a picture with colors
Example
These directions say to color the picture.
📃paper(n.)
纸；纸张▾
Simple meaning
a flat sheet used for writing
Example
I work on a paper that has directions at the top.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

These directions say to ___.
I know how to follow these directions.
I read the directions, and then I ___ the work.

📐 Key Grammar

Present simple tense for routines — "Each day, my class learns about science, reading, and math."
"and then" to show sequence — "I read the directions, and then I start the work."
"say to + verb" (infinitive) — "These directions say to color the picture."

💭 Things to Remember

Following directions helps students show what they know.
There are many kinds of directions: writing, coloring, reading, and circling.
Looking over your work before giving it to your teacher is an important step.
