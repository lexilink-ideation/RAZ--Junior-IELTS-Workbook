Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🌾savanna(n.)
稀树草原▾
Simple meaning
a large, flat area of grassland with few trees, found in Africa
Example
It is the dry season on the African savanna.
💧watering hole(n.)
水坑，饮水处▾
Simple meaning
a place with water where wild animals go to drink
Example
Animals come to the watering hole to drink.
🥤drink(v.)
喝，饮▾
Simple meaning
to take liquid, like water, into your body through your mouth
Example
Some animals come alone to drink.
🐦flock(n.)
（鸟）群▾
Simple meaning
a group of birds that stay and move together
Example
A flock of storks comes to take a drink.
🐕pack(n.)
（群居动物）群▾
Simple meaning
a group of wild animals, such as dogs, that live and travel together
Example
A pack of dogs comes to take a drink.
🐘parade(n.)
（象）队，一列▾
Simple meaning
a long line of animals or people moving together
Example
A parade of elephants comes to take a drink.
🦓herd(n.)
（兽）群▾
Simple meaning
a large group of animals, such as zebras, that live and move together
Example
A herd of zebras comes to take a drink.
🙇bend(v.)
弯腰，俯身▾
Simple meaning
to move part of your body forward and down
Example
A tall giraffe bends to take a drink.
🤿dive(v.)
俯冲，潜入▾
Simple meaning
to jump or go quickly into water
Example
A big hippo dives to take a drink.
🦁hunt(v.)
捕猎▾
Simple meaning
to chase and catch animals for food
Example
Some animals come to drink and hunt.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

A [animal] comes to take a drink.
A [group word] of [animals] comes to take a drink.
Some animals come to drink and [verb].

📐 Key Grammar

Present tense -s verbs for facts: "comes," "bends," "dives," "stops."
Collective nouns for animal groups: a flock of storks, a herd of zebras.
Infinitive "to + verb" shows purpose: "to take a drink," "look for a meal."

💭 Things to Remember

Many different animals share the same watering hole in Africa.
Some animals come alone, and others come together in groups.
Some animals, like lions and hyenas, come to the watering hole to hunt, not just to drink.
