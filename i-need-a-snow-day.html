Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
📌important(adj.)
重要的▾
Simple meaning
something you must not forget because it matters a lot
Example
I have an important math test tomorrow.
😟nervous(adj.)
紧张的▾
Simple meaning
feeling worried or a little afraid about something
Example
I studied for it, but I am still so nervous.
📚studied(v.)
学习了；复习了▾
Simple meaning
looked at and learned something carefully (past tense of "study")
Example
I studied for it, but I am still so nervous.
🚌run(v.)
运行；行驶▾
Simple meaning
(for a bus, train, or car) to move and work normally
Example
Let it snow so much that the buses can't run.
❄️flake(n.)
雪花▾
Simple meaning
one small, soft piece of falling snow
Example
I don't even see one flake.
🚇subways(n.)
地铁▾
Simple meaning
trains that travel under the ground in a city
Example
Let it snow so much that the subways are shut down.
🚗buried(v./adj.)
被埋住的▾
Simple meaning
covered so completely that you cannot see it
Example
Let it snow so much that Dad's car is buried.
🚪blocked(v./adj.)
堵住的▾
Simple meaning
closed so that nothing can go in or out
Example
Let it snow so much that the school doors are blocked.
😌easy(adj.)
容易的▾
Simple meaning
not hard to do
Example
This math test was easy.

Reading Comprehension

Answer the questions about the story.

A · True or False 判断对错

1.The girl has an important math test tomorrow.
2.The girl feels happy and calm about her math test.
3.The girl wishes for a snow day so she won't have to take the test.
4.In the morning, there is a lot of snow outside.
5.The girl thinks the math test was easy after all.

B · Multiple Choice 选择题

1.Why is the girl nervous?
2.What does the girl wish would happen that night?
3.What does the girl say happens to the buses in her wish?
4.What does the girl see when she wakes up in the morning?
5.How does the girl feel about the test at the end of the story?

C · Short Answer 简答题

1.What does the girl need to do tomorrow?

Hint: Look at page 3 — "I have an important ___ test tomorrow."
2.Name two things the girl wishes the snow would stop from working.

Hint: Think about the buses, the subways, Dad's car, and the school doors.
3.Have you ever felt nervous about a test? What did you do?

📐 Key Grammar

Past tense verbs (-ed): "I studied for it."
Modal "can't" for inability: "the buses can't run."
Exclamation sentences for strong feeling: "I need a snow day!"

💭 Things to Remember

The girl feels nervous about her math test, so she wishes hard for a snow day.
In the morning there is no snow at all — not even one flake.
The test turns out easy, and she realizes she did not need to feel so nervous.
