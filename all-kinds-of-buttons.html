🧩 Sentence Patterns

This button is ___.
This ___ button is ___.
It is ___ and ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple Present (be): This button is round.
Adjectives after "is": is small, is shiny, is dark.
"This" for one near thing: This button (not these buttons).

💭 Things to Remember

Buttons come in many shapes: round and square.
Buttons are made of different things: wood and metal.
Buttons can be dark or shiny, small or large.
