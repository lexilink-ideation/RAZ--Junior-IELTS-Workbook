Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
🤝join(v.)
加入▾
Simple meaning
to come together with a group and become part of it
Example
"Come join us!" the two birds call.
📢call(v.)
叫；喊▾
Simple meaning
to say something in a loud voice so others can hear
Example
"Come join us!" the three raccoons call.
🙈behind(prep.)
在...后面▾
Simple meaning
at or to the back of something
Example
Five deer are behind the trees.
➕more(adj.)
更多的▾
Simple meaning
a greater number or amount than before
Example
They see one more bird.
💯all(pron./adj.)
全部；所有▾
Simple meaning
every one of a group; the whole number
Example
How many birds are there in all?
🌳tree(n.)
树▾
Simple meaning
a tall plant with a trunk, branches, and leaves
Example
Two birds are in the tree.
🌊river(n.)
河流▾
Simple meaning
a long, natural stream of water that flows along the land
Example
Two fish are in the river.
🪨cave(n.)
洞穴▾
Simple meaning
a large natural hole in a hill, mountain, or under the ground
Example
Two bears are near their cave.
🕳️hole(n.)
洞；地洞▾
Simple meaning
a hollow space in the ground, often an animal's home
Example
Two mice are in their hole.
🔢count(v.)
数数▾
Simple meaning
to find out how many things there are by adding them up
Example
Readers count how many friends there are in all.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

[Number] [animals] are in/near the [place].
They see/hear [number] more [animals].
"Come join us!" the [animals] call.

📐 Key Grammar

Plural nouns after numbers: Two birds, Three raccoons, Five deer
Simple present tense for repeated action: are, see, hear, call
Direct speech uses quotation marks: "Come join us!"

💭 Things to Remember

Groups of animal friends get bigger when more friends join them.
We can count "how many in all" by adding the two groups together.
Animals live in different places: trees, holes, rivers, and caves.
