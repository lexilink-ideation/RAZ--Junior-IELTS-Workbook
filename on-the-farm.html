🧩 Sentence Patterns

A ___ is in a ___.

Animals are on a farm.

📐 Key Grammar

Article "a": use "a" before one thing. — A dog is in a house.
in / on: "in" = inside; "on" = on top. — in a barn / on a hill.
is / are: one animal → "is"; many animals → "are". — Animals are on a farm.

💭 Things to Remember

Farm animals live in different places.
Cows sleep in a barn; pigs play in the mud.
Ducks like the pond; sheep like the hill.
