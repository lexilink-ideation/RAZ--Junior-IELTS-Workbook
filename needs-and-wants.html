Vocabulary

Tap each card to learn the word! 点击单词卡查看意思！
💧need(n./v.)
需要▾
Simple meaning
something you must have to live; to have to have something
Example
We need food, water, and air.
🎁want(n./v.)
想要▾
Simple meaning
something you would like to have but don't have to have
Example
We want things that make us happy.
🔥warm(adj.)
温暖的▾
Simple meaning
having a comfortable heat, not cold
Example
Erin needs a warm coat for the winter.
🧥coat(n.)
外套▾
Simple meaning
a piece of clothing you wear outside to stay warm
Example
She wants a long red coat.
⛈️storm(n.)
暴风雨▾
Simple meaning
very bad, windy, rainy weather
Example
Erin needs to stay safe from the storm.
🛡️safe(adj.)
安全的▾
Simple meaning
not in danger; protected from harm
Example
Erin needs to stay safe from the storm.
🌬️breathe(v.)
呼吸▾
Simple meaning
to take air into your body and let it out
Example
Jacob needs air to breathe.
🍃wind(n.)
风▾
Simple meaning
air that moves outside
Example
He wants some wind to fly his kite.
🪁kite(n.)
风筝▾
Simple meaning
a toy that flies in the sky on a string
Example
He wants some wind to fly his kite.
👕clothing(n.)
衣物▾
Simple meaning
things people wear, like shirts and coats
Example
We need a home and clothing, too.

🧩 Sentence Patterns

___ needs ___ to ___.
___ wants ___.
We all need things. We all want things.

📐 Key Grammar

Third person singular -s: "Erin needs...", "Jacob wants..."
Infinitive of purpose "to + verb": "water to drink", "air to breathe"
Contrast connector: "She needs a warm coat. She wants a long red coat."

💭 Things to Remember

A need is something you must have, like food, water, air, a home, and clothing.
A want is something you would like to have but do not have to have.
Erin and Jacob each have needs and wants that are different from each other.
