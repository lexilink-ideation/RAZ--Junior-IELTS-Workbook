🗳️vote(v.)
投票▾
Simple meaning
to choose someone or something by marking a ballot
Example
Election Day is a day when people vote.
🧑‍💼leaders(n.)
领导者▾
Simple meaning
people who are in charge, like a president or mayor
Example
They can vote to keep or change leaders.
📋rules(n.)
规则▾
Simple meaning
instructions that tell people what they can and cannot do
Example
They can vote to make new rules or laws.
⚖️laws(n.)
法律▾
Simple meaning
official rules made by a government that everyone must follow
Example
They can vote to make new rules or laws.
🚪booths(n.)
投票亭▾
Simple meaning
small, private spaces where people vote
Example
People use booths to vote on Election Day.
🚶‍♀️line(n.)
队伍▾
Simple meaning
a row of people waiting for their turn
Example
People wait in line to vote on Election Day.
✅check in(v.)
登记▾
Simple meaning
to tell workers you have arrived so they can find your name
Example
People check in to vote on Election Day.
⭐stickers(n.)
贴纸▾
Simple meaning
small pieces of paper with glue on the back, used as a reward
Example
People get stickers for voting on Election Day.
🔢count(v.)
清点▾
Simple meaning

Example
People count the votes on Election Day.
🔄change(v.)

Simple meaning

Example

🧩 Sentence Patterns

People ___ on Election Day.
They can vote to ___.
People wait in line to ___.

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present for facts and routines: "People vote on Election Day."
Infinitive of purpose "to + verb": "People wait in line to vote."
Modal "can" for possibility: "They can vote to keep or change leaders."

💭 Things to Remember

Voting lets people choose or change leaders, and make new rules or laws.
On Election Day, people travel to vote, wait in line, check in, and use booths.
After voting, people get a sticker, and workers count the votes.
