🧩 Sentence Patterns

Some people in ___ think a ___ brings good luck.
Many people believe that ___.
Is there something that you think brings you good luck?

📐 Key Grammar

Simple present tense for facts/beliefs: "Some people think an elephant brings good luck."
Prepositional phrases of place with "in": "in India," "in China," "in England."
Verb pattern "think (that) + clause": "Some people in India think (that) an elephant brings good luck."

💭 Things to Remember

Different countries have different symbols for good luck: elephants in India, clovers in Ireland, horseshoes in England.
Some lucky symbols belong to one country, like red lanterns in China and rabbit's feet in the United States.
Some things, like rainbows and falling stars, are believed to bring good luck to people all around the world.
